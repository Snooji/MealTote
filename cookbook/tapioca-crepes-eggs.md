# Brazilian Tapioca Crepes with Scrambled Eggs & Cheese

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Breakfast** · Brazilian · 25 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Street-cart tapiocas from northeast Brazil: moistened tapioca flour sifted into a hot pan sets into a chewy, gluten-free crepe in seconds, then gets folded around soft eggs, melting mozzarella and tomato.

## Ingredients

- 2⅔ cups tapioca flour
- 8 eggs
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 cup grape tomatoes, chopped
- 2 green onions, sliced

From the pantry: ½ cup water, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp pepper

## Steps

1. Sprinkle the water and a pinch of salt over the tapioca flour and rub it in with your fingers until it's evenly damp, like wet sand. A squeezed handful should hold together but crumble when poked; add water a teaspoon at a time if needed. Push it through a sieve to break up lumps.
2. Whisk the eggs with the rest of the salt and the pepper. Scramble them softly in 1 tbsp butter over medium-low heat, 3 to 4 minutes, and fold in the green onions. Keep warm.
3. Heat a dry 8-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high. Sift about ⅓ cup of the tapioca evenly over the bottom, press the edges with a spatula, and cook until it holds together as one disc, 30 to 45 seconds. Flip and cook 20 seconds more.
4. Brush the crepe with a little butter, spoon eggs, cheese and tomatoes over half, fold it over and press until the cheese melts. Wipe the pan and repeat to make 8.

**Tip:** Many Brazilian markets sell pre-hydrated 'goma de tapioca' in a tub, which skips the first step. Serve the crepes right away; they turn rubbery as they cool.

Adapted from [Easy and Delish](https://www.easyanddelish.com/brazilian-breakfast-and-tapioca-crepes-with-cheese/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer not recommended. Moistened tapioca flour keeps in a sealed tub in the fridge for a week; make crepes fresh.
