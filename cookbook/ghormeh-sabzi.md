# Ghormeh Sabzi (Persian Herb & Beef Stew with Kidney Beans) over Saffron Rice

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Dinner** · Persian · 150 min · serves 6 · skill 2/3

> Iran's beloved national stew: chunks of beef slow-cooked with a mountain of fried parsley, cilantro, spinach and fenugreek, red kidney beans and sour lemon until dark green, deep and tangy. Served over fluffy saffron basmati.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless chuck, in 1½-inch cubes
- 2 cans (15 oz) kidney beans, rinsed
- 2 bunches parsley, finely chopped
- 1 bunch cilantro, finely chopped
- 10 oz spinach, finely chopped
- 1 bunch green onions, finely chopped
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 lemons, juiced
- 2 cups basmati rice
- a pinch of saffron

From the pantry: 5 tbsp oil, 2 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves, 1 tsp turmeric, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Season the beef with salt and pepper and brown it in 2 tbsp oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat, in batches, 6 to 8 minutes. Add the onion and turmeric and cook 3 minutes more.
2. In a large skillet, cook the herbs, spinach and green onions in the remaining 3 tbsp oil over medium heat, stirring often, until they shrink, darken to a deep forest green and smell toasty, 15 to 20 minutes. Stir in the fenugreek for the last minute.
3. Add the herbs to the beef with 4 cups water and 1½ tsp salt. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer gently until the beef is very tender, about 1½ hours.
4. Stir in the kidney beans and half the lemon juice and simmer uncovered 20 minutes so the stew thickens. Taste and add more lemon and salt until it is tart and savory.
5. While the stew finishes, steep the saffron in 2 tbsp hot water. Cook the rice, then drizzle the saffron water over the top and fluff. Serve the stew over the rice.

**Tip:** Traditionally the sourness comes from 4 dried limes (limoo amani), pierced and simmered in the stew; look for them at Middle Eastern markets and use them in place of the lemon. Frying the herbs low and long is the step that makes it taste right.

## Storing

Fridge 5 days · freezer up to 4 months. It tastes better the next day. Reheat gently in a covered pot with a splash of water.
