# Buffalo Chicken Rice Bowls with Ranch Yogurt

> Juicy seared chicken tossed in tangy buffalo sauce over rice with cool cucumber, carrots, celery and tomatoes, then drizzled with a creamy ranch yogurt and feta. Wing-night flavor in a bowl.

## Ingredients

- 2¼ lb boneless chicken breasts, in 1-inch pieces
- ½ cup buffalo wing sauce
- ¾ cup rice
- 1 cup shredded carrots
- 2 celery ribs, thinly sliced
- ½ English cucumber, diced
- 1 cup grape tomatoes, halved
- ¾ cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp ranch dressing
- ⅓ cup crumbled feta or blue cheese
- 3 green onions, sliced

From the pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp dried dill, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Cook the rice according to the package. Toss the chicken with the garlic powder, paprika, ½ tsp salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the chicken in a single layer, turning once, until browned and 165°F inside, 7 to 9 minutes.
3. Take the pan off the heat, pour in the buffalo sauce and toss until every piece is glossy.
4. Stir the yogurt, ranch, dill and a splash of water into a pourable sauce.
5. Divide the rice among bowls and top with the buffalo chicken, carrots, celery, cucumber and tomatoes. Drizzle with ranch yogurt and finish with feta and green onions.

**Tip:** Toss the chicken in the sauce off the heat; buffalo sauce is mostly butter and vinegar and turns greasy if it boils.

Adapted from [Lauren Fit Foodie](https://laurenfitfoodie.com/buffalo-chicken-rice-bowls/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 2 months. Pack the vegetables and sauce separately. Reheat the chicken and rice 2 minutes, then add the cold toppings.
