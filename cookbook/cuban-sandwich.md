# Cuban Sandwiches

## Ingredients

- 4 Cuban or hoagie rolls
- 10 oz roast pork, sliced (or leftover mojo pork)
- 9 oz sliced ham
- 8 slices Swiss
- dill pickle slices
- 2 tbsp butter

From the pantry: Yellow mustard, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. If roasting the pork fresh: season and roast at 425°F to 145°F, 20 minutes. Rest and slice thin.
2. Split the rolls and spread both sides with mustard.
3. Layer Swiss, ham, pork, pickles and more Swiss.
4. Butter the outsides and press in a hot skillet under a heavy pan, 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden and the cheese melts.
5. Slice on the diagonal.

**Tip:** No panini press? Weigh it down with a cast-iron pan and a can.


## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer not recommended. Assemble fresh. Re-press leftovers in a skillet.
