# Southern Pimento Cheese & Tomato Sandwiches

**Lunch** · Southern & soul food · 15 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> The Southern spread everyone fights over: sharp cheddar, cream cheese and sweet pimientos whipped with a little mayo and cayenne, spread thick on bread with ripe tomato.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
- 2 oz cream cheese, softened
- 4 oz jar diced pimientos, drained
- 8 slices whole wheat bread
- 2 Roma tomatoes, sliced
- ½ cucumber, sliced, to serve

From the pantry: ⅓ cup mayo, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp yellow mustard, ¼ tsp garlic powder, ⅛ tsp cayenne, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Beat the cream cheese, mayo, Worcestershire, mustard, garlic powder and cayenne with a spatula or hand mixer until smooth and fluffy.
2. Fold in the cheddar and the drained pimientos until evenly mixed. Taste and add salt and pepper.
3. Chill for 15 minutes if you have time; it firms up and the flavors come together.
4. Spread the pimento cheese thickly on 4 slices of bread, add tomato slices and a pinch of salt, and top with the remaining bread.
5. Cut in half and serve with cucumber slices on the side.

**Tip:** Shred the cheddar from a block on the small holes of a box grater. Pre-shredded cheese is coated in starch and makes the spread dry.

Adapted from [Spend With Pennies](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/pimento-cheese/).

## Storing

Fridge 5 days · freezer not recommended. The pimento cheese keeps 5 days covered in the fridge. Make sandwiches fresh.
