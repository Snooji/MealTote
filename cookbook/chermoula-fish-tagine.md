# Chermoula Fish Tagine with Potatoes, Peppers & Olives

**Dinner** · Moroccan · 60 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Thick white fish marinated in chermoula (herbs, garlic, cumin, paprika and lemon), braised on a bed of potatoes, peppers and tomatoes with salty olives.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb thick cod fillets, in 4 pieces
- 1½ lb baby potatoes, sliced ¼ inch
- 2 bell peppers, in strips
- 4 Roma tomatoes, sliced
- 1 bunch cilantro
- ½ bunch parsley
- 5 garlic cloves
- 2 lemons
- ½ cup green or kalamata olives
- ½ baguette, for serving

From the pantry: ⅓ cup olive oil, 2 tsp paprika, 2 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp turmeric, ¼ tsp cayenne, salt

## Steps

1. Make chermoula: blend the cilantro, parsley, garlic, juice of 1½ lemons, olive oil, paprika, cumin, turmeric, cayenne and 1 tsp salt to a loose paste. Coat the fish with a third of it and chill 20 minutes.
2. Toss the potatoes, peppers and half the tomatoes with half the remaining chermoula and a pinch of salt.
3. Spread the potatoes in a wide braiser or large skillet with a lid, top with the peppers and tomatoes, add ½ cup water, cover and cook over medium-low until the potatoes are nearly tender, 20 minutes.
4. Lay the fish on top with the rest of the tomatoes, the olives and the last of the chermoula. Cover and cook until the fish flakes easily, 10 to 12 minutes.
5. Squeeze over the remaining lemon and serve from the pan with bread.

**Tip:** A quarter of a preserved lemon, rind chopped, scattered over with the olives gives the tagine its classic Moroccan tang; find it in jars near the olives.

Adapted from [My Moroccan Food](https://www.mymoroccanfood.com/home/oven-baked-fish-with-chermoula-and-preserved-lemon-moroccan-fish-tagine).

## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer not recommended. Reheat covered over low, 8 minutes, until just hot.
