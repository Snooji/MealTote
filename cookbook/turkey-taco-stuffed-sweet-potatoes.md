# Turkey Taco Stuffed Sweet Potatoes

> Fluffy baked sweet potatoes split open and loaded with saucy taco-spiced turkey, black beans, melted cheddar, salsa and a cool dollop of yogurt. Sweet, smoky and seriously filling.

## Ingredients

- 4 small sweet potatoes (1½ lb)
- 2 lb 99% lean ground turkey
- ½ cup crushed tomatoes
- ½ cup black beans, rinsed
- ½ cup shredded cheddar
- ¾ cup plain Greek yogurt
- ½ cup chunky salsa
- 3 green onions, sliced

From the pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp chili powder, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp oregano, salt

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Prick the sweet potatoes, set them on a foil-lined sheet and bake until completely soft, 45 to 50 minutes (or microwave 8 to 10 minutes, turning once).
2. Meanwhile heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and brown the turkey, breaking it up, 6 to 7 minutes.
3. Stir in the spices and 1 tsp salt for 1 minute, then add the crushed tomatoes, beans and ½ cup water. Simmer until thick and saucy, 5 to 8 minutes.
4. Split the potatoes, fluff the insides with a fork and pile in the turkey. Top with cheddar and broil 2 minutes to melt.
5. Finish with yogurt, salsa and green onions.

**Tip:** 99% lean turkey can dry out, so don't skip the tomatoes and water; the saucy simmer keeps it juicy.

Adapted from [Andie Mitchell](https://www.andiemitchell.com/stuffed-sweet-potatoes-with-taco-seasoned-ground-turkey/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Store potatoes and filling together. Microwave 2 to 3 minutes and add the yogurt and salsa after.
