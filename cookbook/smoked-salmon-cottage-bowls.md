# Everything-Bagel Cottage Cheese Bowls with Smoked Salmon & Jammy Eggs

**Breakfast** · American comfort · 15 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> All the flavors of a lox bagel without the bagel: creamy cottage cheese topped with silky smoked salmon, a jammy egg, cucumber, tomatoes, avocado and a big shake of everything seasoning.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups low-fat cottage cheese
- 4 large eggs
- 4 oz smoked salmon, torn
- 1 English cucumber, diced
- 1 cup grape tomatoes, halved
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 4 tsp everything bagel seasoning

From the pantry: 1 tsp lemon juice, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Bring a small pot of water to a boil. Lower in the eggs and cook 7 minutes for jammy yolks, then move them to a bowl of ice water for 3 minutes and peel.
2. Spoon 1 cup of cottage cheese into each of 4 bowls and season with a little pepper.
3. Top each with cucumber, tomatoes, a quarter of the salmon and some avocado. Squeeze a little lemon over the avocado.
4. Halve the eggs, set two halves on each bowl, and finish with green onions and a generous teaspoon of everything seasoning.

**Tip:** Boil a dozen eggs at once on Sunday. Peeled jammy eggs keep 4 days in the fridge, and these bowls then take 3 minutes to put together.

Adapted from [The Chef Out West](https://thechefoutwest.com/high-protein-smoked-salmon-breakfast-bowl/).

## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer not recommended. Store the parts separately and assemble just before eating; slice the avocado fresh.
