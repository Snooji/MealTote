# Argentine Chicken Empanadas with Peppers & Egg

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Lunch** · Argentinian · 90 min · serves 6 · skill 2/3

> Golden baked turnovers filled with tender poached chicken thighs, sweet peppers, paprika and oregano, with chopped egg and green onion the way Argentine grandmothers make them.

## Ingredients

- 20 empanada dough discs, thawed
- 1½ lb boneless chicken thighs
- 2 large onions, chopped
- 1 red and 1 green bell pepper, chopped
- 4 green onions, sliced
- 3 eggs (2 hard-boiled, 1 for brushing)
- ½ cup green olives, chopped (optional)

From the pantry: 3 tbsp oil, 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp sweet paprika, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp ground cumin, ¼ tsp cayenne, 2 bay leaves, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Simmer the chicken with the bay leaves and 2½ cups water until cooked through, about 25 minutes. Lift out, chop finely and keep ½ cup of the broth.
2. Cook the onions and peppers in the oil over medium heat until soft but not browned, 8 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste until brick red, 1 minute, then the paprika, oregano, cumin and cayenne.
3. Add the chicken and reserved broth and simmer, stirring, until almost dry, about 10 minutes. Season well with salt and pepper, stir in the green onions, and chill the filling until cold, at least 1 hour.
4. Heat the oven to 400°F. Chop the hard-boiled eggs. Put 2 tbsp filling in the center of each disc with a little egg and olive, brush the edge with water, fold and pinch shut, then crimp with a fork or twist into a rope edge.
5. Set on parchment-lined sheets, brush with the beaten egg and bake until deep golden, 18 to 22 minutes. Cool for 5 minutes before eating.

**Tip:** The filling must be cold before you fill the discs or the dough tears and leaks. Empanada discs are in the freezer case near the Latin foods; puff pastry discs make flakier empanadas.

Adapted from [Gaby's Kitchen](https://www.gabyskitchen.com/recipes/argentinian-chicken-empanadas).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Freeze unbaked empanadas on a tray, then bag; bake from frozen at 400°F for 25 to 30 minutes. Reheat baked ones at 350°F for 10 minutes.
