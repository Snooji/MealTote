# Salsa Verde Double-Chicken Burrito Bowls

## Ingredients

- 2½ lb boneless chicken breasts
- 1 cup salsa verde
- ½ onion, diced
- ¾ cup rice
- ½ can black beans, rinsed
- 1 cup corn
- 1 Roma tomato, diced
- ½ bunch cilantro, chopped
- 2 limes
- ⅔ cup plain Greek yogurt
- ½ cup shredded Mexican cheese
- ½ avocado, diced

From the pantry: 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp oregano, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt

## Steps

1. Season the chicken with cumin, oregano, garlic powder and ½ tsp salt. Put it in a pot with the onion and salsa verde, cover and simmer gently until it shreds easily, 20 to 25 minutes (or 4 hours on low in a slow cooker).
2. Shred the chicken in the pot and simmer uncovered until the sauce clings, 5 minutes.
3. Cook the rice and toss it with half the cilantro and the juice of 1 lime. Warm the beans and corn together.
4. Stir the yogurt with lime juice and salt; mix the tomato with a little cilantro and salt for pico.
5. Build bowls with rice, beans and corn, a big scoop of salsa verde chicken, cheese, pico, avocado and crema.

**Tip:** Double the chicken and freeze half in its sauce; it's a ready-made filling for tacos and bowls.

Adapted from [Skinnytaste](https://www.skinnytaste.com/easiest-crock-pot-salsa-verde-chicken/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Store the toppings separately. Reheat the chicken, rice and beans 2 minutes.
