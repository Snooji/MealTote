# Turkey Sausage & Egg Breakfast Stuffed Peppers

**Breakfast** · American comfort · 55 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Sweet bell pepper halves baked into edible bowls, filled with sage-seasoned turkey sausage, mushrooms, spinach and a fluffy egg custard under a cap of melted cheddar. Colorful, low-carb and very meal-prep friendly.

## Ingredients

- 4 bell peppers, halved through the stem and seeded
- 12 oz 93% lean ground turkey
- 4 large eggs
- 2 cups liquid egg whites
- 4 oz sliced baby bella mushrooms, chopped
- 3 oz baby spinach, chopped
- ½ cup shredded sharp cheddar

From the pantry: 1 tsp oil, ½ tsp dried sage, ½ tsp garlic powder, ¼ tsp red pepper flakes, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Set the pepper halves cut side up in a 9x13-inch baking dish, add 2 tbsp water to the dish, cover with foil and bake 10 minutes to soften.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the turkey with the sage, garlic powder, pepper flakes and ½ tsp salt, breaking it up, until browned, 6 minutes. Add the mushrooms and cook until their liquid evaporates, 4 minutes. Stir in the spinach to wilt and let cool slightly.
3. Whisk the eggs and egg whites with a pinch of salt and pepper, then stir in the turkey mixture.
4. Spoon the filling into the peppers (it will be full), and bake uncovered until the eggs are set in the center, 25 to 30 minutes.
5. Sprinkle with cheddar and bake 3 to 4 minutes more until melted. Let rest 5 minutes before serving 2 halves per person.

**Tip:** Cut the peppers through the stem, not across, so each half has a flat bottom and a little wall at the stem end to hold the egg in.

Adapted from [Skinnytaste](https://www.skinnytaste.com/breakfast-stuffed-peppers/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer not recommended. Refrigerate in a covered container; microwave 1½ to 2 minutes, or reheat at 350°F for 15 minutes.
