# Sheet-Pan Chicken Sausage, Broccoli & Pepper Egg Bake

**Breakfast** · American comfort · 40 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Roasted chicken sausage, charred broccoli and sweet peppers on one pan, with seasoned egg whites poured around them and whole eggs baked into little wells. A hands-off weekday breakfast with almost no cleanup.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz fully cooked chicken sausage (4 links), sliced
- 3 cups liquid egg whites
- 4 large eggs
- 12 oz broccoli, in small florets
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- ½ red or yellow onion, diced

From the pantry: 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp dried oregano, salt, pepper, cooking spray

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a rimmed half-sheet pan with parchment and coat with cooking spray.
2. Toss the broccoli, peppers and onion with the oil, garlic powder, paprika, oregano, ½ tsp salt and pepper. Spread on the pan and roast 10 minutes.
3. Add the sausage slices, stir, and roast 8 minutes more, until the broccoli is browned at the tips.
4. Lower the oven to 375°F. Spread everything into an even layer, whisk the egg whites with a pinch of salt and pour them over. Make 4 wells and crack an egg into each.
5. Bake until the whites are set and the yolks are as done as you like, 10 to 14 minutes. Cut into 4 portions, each with an egg.

**Tip:** Lowering the oven before adding the eggs keeps the whites tender instead of bubbly and tough at the edges.

Adapted from [Eggland's Best](https://www.egglandsbest.com/recipes/sheet-pan-breakfast-with-eggs-sausage-and-sweet-potatoes).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer not recommended. Refrigerate portions; microwave 1½ minutes. For meal prep, bake the yolks fully set.
