# Chicken BLT Ranch Wraps

**Lunch** · American comfort · 20 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Everything you love in a BLT plus shredded rotisserie chicken, cheddar and cool ranch, rolled tight in a big flour tortilla. Great for packed lunches.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups shredded rotisserie chicken
- 6 slices thick-cut bacon
- 4 large flour tortillas
- 1 romaine heart, shredded
- 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
- ½ cup shredded cheddar
- ¼ cup ranch dressing

From the pantry: ¼ tsp black pepper

## Steps

1. Lay the bacon on a foil-lined sheet pan and bake at 400°F until crisp, 15 to 18 minutes. Drain and crumble or break into pieces.
2. Toss the chicken with half the ranch and the pepper.
3. Warm the tortillas for 15 seconds in the microwave so they roll without cracking.
4. Spread a little of the remaining ranch down the center of each tortilla. Layer lettuce, chicken, bacon, tomato and cheddar, keeping the filling in a compact log.
5. Fold in the sides, roll up tightly and cut in half on the diagonal.

**Tip:** Put the lettuce down first, next to the tortilla. It works as a barrier so the wrap doesn't get soggy if you pack it ahead.

Adapted from [Taste of Home](https://www.tasteofhome.com/recipes/blt-wraps/).

## Storing

Fridge 1 days · freezer not recommended. Wrap tightly in foil and refrigerate up to 1 day.
