# Eggs in Purgatory with Turkey Sausage & Egg Whites

**Breakfast** · Italian · 40 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> The Italian cousin of shakshuka: a spicy, fennel-scented tomato sauce loaded with turkey sausage, with eggs and extra egg whites poached right in it, then showered with Parmesan and basil. Mop it up with crusty toast.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb 93% lean ground turkey
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- ½ onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 large eggs
- 2 cups liquid egg whites
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- a few basil leaves, torn
- 3 oz sourdough, sliced and toasted

From the pantry: 2 tsp olive oil, 1 tsp fennel seed, 1 tsp dried oregano, ½ tsp red pepper flakes, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a 12-inch skillet with a lid over medium-high heat. Add the turkey, fennel seed, oregano, pepper flakes and ¾ tsp salt and cook, breaking it up, until browned, 6 to 7 minutes.
2. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook until soft, about 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds, then the crushed tomatoes. Simmer, stirring now and then, until slightly thickened, 10 minutes.
3. Lower the heat to medium-low. Pour the egg whites in a thin stream over the sauce in a few places and let them set slightly, 2 minutes.
4. Make 4 wells with a spoon and crack an egg into each. Season with salt and pepper, cover and cook until the whites are set and the yolks are still soft, 5 to 7 minutes.
5. Sprinkle with Parmesan and basil and serve straight from the pan with the toast.

**Tip:** Check the eggs often near the end; a lid traps heat and the yolks go from runny to firm in about a minute.

Adapted from [From A Chef's Kitchen](https://www.fromachefskitchen.com/eggs-in-purgatory-italian-sausage/).

## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer not recommended. Refrigerate the sauce without eggs for up to 4 days; reheat and poach fresh eggs in it.
