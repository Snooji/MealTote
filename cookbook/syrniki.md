# Syrniki (Ukrainian Farmer's Cheese Pancakes)

![Syrniki (Ukrainian Farmer's Cheese Pancakes)](../photos/syrniki.jpg)

**Breakfast** · Russian & Ukrainian · 30 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Golden, crisp-edged little pancakes made mostly of tangy farmer's cheese, creamy inside and lightly sweet, served with sour cream and warm berries.

## Ingredients

- 15 oz farmer's cheese
- 3 eggs
- ½ cup sour cream, to serve
- 8 oz frozen berries, warmed, to serve

From the pantry: ¾ cup flour plus ½ cup for dredging, 3 tbsp sugar, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp baking soda, 1 tsp vinegar, ½ cup raisins, ¼ cup oil

## Steps

1. Mash the farmer's cheese with the eggs, sugar and salt until fairly smooth (a few small lumps are fine). Stir in ¾ cup flour.
2. Stir the baking soda and vinegar together in a cup so it fizzes, then mix it into the batter along with the raisins.
3. Warm the berries in a small pan over low heat until juicy, 5 minutes.
4. Put the dredging flour in a shallow bowl. Drop heaped tablespoons of batter into it, dust the tops and, with floured hands, shape into ½-inch-thick patties.
5. Fry in 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium heat until deep golden, 3 to 4 minutes per side, adding oil between batches. Serve warm with sour cream and berries.

**Tip:** The cheese must be dry; if yours is wet, press it in a towel for 10 minutes. True tvorog is sold at Eastern European markets, but supermarket farmer's cheese is the easiest match.

Adapted from [Natasha's Kitchen](https://natashaskitchen.com/ukrainian-syrniki-recipe/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 2 months. Refrigerate or freeze cooked syrniki. Reheat in a skillet over medium-low or at 350°F for 8 minutes.
