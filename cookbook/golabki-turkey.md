# Gołąbki (Turkey & Rice Cabbage Rolls in Tomato Sauce)

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Dinner** · Polish · 105 min · serves 5 · skill 3/3

> Soft cabbage leaves wrapped around a juicy, marjoram-scented turkey and rice filling, simmered in broth and finished in a silky tomato sauce. Lighter than the classic pork, just as comforting.

## Ingredients

- 1 head green cabbage (~2 lb)
- 1½ lb ground turkey
- ⅔ cup rice
- 1 onion, grated
- 1 egg
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 28 oz can crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp butter
- chopped parsley
- ½ cup sour cream, to serve
- 1½ lb baby potatoes, boiled, to serve

From the pantry: 1 tbsp dried marjoram, 1½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, 1 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tsp sugar

## Steps

1. Cook the rice in 1⅓ cups water until just tender, about 15 minutes, and let cool. Cut out the cabbage core, lower the head into a big pot of boiling salted water and simmer until the outer leaves loosen, 8 to 10 minutes. Peel off 10 to 12 leaves (return the head briefly if the inner ones resist) and shave the thick ribs flat.
2. Mix the turkey, rice, onion, egg, garlic, marjoram, 1 tsp salt, pepper and half the parsley with your hands until combined.
3. Put about ½ cup filling at the stem end of each leaf, fold in the sides and roll up snugly. Line a deep 12-inch pan or Dutch oven with a few torn leftover leaves and set the rolls in seam-side down.
4. Pour in the broth, bring to a simmer, cover and cook over medium-low for 30 minutes. Start the potatoes boiling while they cook.
5. Lift the rolls out. Boil the broth until reduced to about 1 cup, then stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, sugar, butter and ½ tsp salt and simmer 5 minutes. Return the rolls, spoon sauce over and simmer, covered, 10 minutes more, until 165°F in the center. Serve with potatoes, sour cream and parsley.

**Tip:** Classic gołąbki use ground pork or pork and beef; with turkey, don't skip the grated onion and egg, which keep the filling moist. Savoy cabbage leaves are even more pliable if you can find them.

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Refrigerate or freeze in their sauce. Reheat covered at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes, or gently on the stove.
