# Tamago Sando (Japanese Egg Salad Sandwiches)

**Breakfast** · Japanese · 30 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> The cult konbini sandwich: creamy, gently sweet egg salad made rich with mayo and milk, pressed between pillowy buttered bread with the crusts cut off. Simple and soft, and you'll want another one.

## Ingredients

- 6 large eggs
- 8 slices soft brioche or milk bread
- 2 tbsp softened butter

From the pantry: ¼ cup mayonnaise, 4 tsp milk, ½ tsp sugar, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp black pepper

## Steps

1. Cover the eggs with cold water by an inch, bring to a boil over medium heat and boil for 12 minutes. Move them to ice water until completely cold, then peel.
2. Mash the eggs with a fork into small, even pieces. Stir in the sugar, salt, pepper and milk, then the mayonnaise, until creamy. Taste and add salt if it needs it.
3. Spread a thin layer of butter on one side of every slice of bread; it keeps the bread from going soggy.
4. Spread a thick layer of egg salad on 4 slices and top with the remaining slices, buttered side down.
5. Set a plate on top of the sandwiches for 5 minutes to press them. Trim off the crusts with a sharp knife and cut each sandwich in half.

**Tip:** Japanese Kewpie mayo, made with only egg yolks and rice vinegar, gives the true konbini flavor. Shokupan from an Asian bakery is the classic bread.

Adapted from [Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/japanese-egg-sandwich/).

## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer not recommended. Wrap tightly and refrigerate; best eaten within a day.
