# Somali Turkey Sambusa with Green Chili Sauce

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Lunch** · Kenyan & East African · 60 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Crackly fried triangles stuffed with cumin-and-cardamom-spiced ground turkey, fresh herbs and chili, with a zippy cilantro-lime dip and cucumber salad. Classically made with beef.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey
- 10 egg roll wrappers, halved into strips
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 jalapeños (2 minced, 1 for the sauce)
- 4 garlic cloves (3 minced, 1 for the sauce)
- 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
- 2 limes
- 1 English cucumber, diced
- 2 Roma tomatoes, diced

From the pantry: oil for frying, 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp ground cardamom, ¼ tsp cinnamon, 3 tbsp flour, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium-high. Cook the turkey and onion, breaking it up, until no pink remains, 6 to 7 minutes. Add the minced garlic and jalapeño and the spices, cook 2 minutes, season well and stir in half the cilantro. Cool 15 minutes.
2. Mix the flour with 3 tbsp water into a paste. Fold each wrapper strip into a cone, fill with 2 tbsp filling, fold over into a triangle and seal the flap with the paste.
3. Heat 1½ inches of oil to 350°F. Fry the sambusa in batches until blistered and golden, 3 to 4 minutes, turning once. Drain on a rack.
4. Blend the remaining cilantro, jalapeño and garlic with the juice of 1½ limes, 2 tbsp water and salt for the sauce. Toss the cucumber and tomatoes with the last lime juice and salt.
5. Serve the sambusa hot with the green sauce and salad.

**Tip:** To bake instead, brush the triangles with oil and bake at 425°F for 15 to 18 minutes, flipping once. Ground beef or lamb is the Somali classic.

Adapted from [Gimme Delicious](https://gimmedelicious.com/beef-sambusa/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 3 months. Freeze uncooked sambusa on a tray, then bag; fry from frozen 1 minute longer. Re-crisp cooked ones at 400°F for 6 to 8 minutes.
