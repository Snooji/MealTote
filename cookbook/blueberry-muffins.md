# Blueberry Oat Muffins with Cinnamon Sugar

**Breakfast** · American comfort · 40 min · serves 6 · skill 1/3

> Tender, domed muffins made moist with Greek yogurt and hearty with oats, bursting with blueberries under a crackly cinnamon-sugar top. Twelve muffins, two each.

## Ingredients

- 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1½ cups fresh blueberries
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- ½ cup milk
- 6 tbsp butter, melted

From the pantry: ⅔ cup sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda, 1 tsp vanilla, 1 tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp salt

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a 12-cup muffin tin. Whisk the flour, oats, baking powder, baking soda, salt and ½ tsp cinnamon in a large bowl.
2. In another bowl whisk the eggs, all but 2 tbsp of the sugar, the yogurt, milk, melted butter and vanilla until smooth.
3. Fold the wet ingredients into the dry with a spatula until just a few streaks of flour remain, then fold in the blueberries. Lumps are fine; overmixing makes tough muffins.
4. Divide the batter among the cups; they will be full. Mix the reserved 2 tbsp sugar with the remaining ½ tsp cinnamon and sprinkle over the tops.
5. Bake 18 to 22 minutes, until golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean. Cool 5 minutes in the tin, then move to a rack.

**Tip:** Starting at 400°F gives the muffins a quick rise and tall domes. If you use frozen blueberries, add them straight from the freezer so they don't streak the batter.

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 3 months. Keep airtight at room temperature 2 days or chilled 3. Freeze individually; microwave 20 to 30 seconds.
