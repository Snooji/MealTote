# Honey Ricotta Toast with Berries & Pistachios

> Thick slices of crisp sourdough piled with whipped lemony ricotta, juicy blueberries, salty pistachios and a generous drizzle of honey.

## Ingredients

- 8 slices sourdough
- 15 oz whole-milk ricotta
- 1 lemon, zested
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 pint fresh blueberries
- ⅓ cup shelled pistachios, chopped

From the pantry: 1 tbsp olive oil, flaky salt

## Steps

1. Whip the ricotta with the lemon zest and a pinch of salt with a fork or whisk for 1 minute, until fluffy.
2. Brush the bread lightly with olive oil and toast until golden and crisp, in a toaster or under the broiler, 2 to 3 minutes per side.
3. Spread each slice thickly with the ricotta.
4. Top with the blueberries and pistachios, drizzle with the honey and finish with a pinch of flaky salt.

**Tip:** Toast the bread properly dark. A sturdy, crunchy slice holds the creamy ricotta without going soft.


## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer not recommended. Store the whipped ricotta covered up to 3 days and assemble toasts fresh.
