# Dan Bing (Taiwanese Egg Crepe Rolls with Cheese & Scallion)

![Dan Bing (Taiwanese Egg Crepe Rolls with Cheese & Scallion)](../photos/taiwanese-dan-bing.jpg)

**Breakfast** · Taiwanese · 25 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> The roll-up you'll find at every Taiwanese breakfast shop: a soft, chewy crepe with a layer of scallion egg and melty cheese, sliced into pieces and drizzled with sweet soy. Fast, cheap and very good with coffee or soy milk.

## Ingredients

- 8 eggs
- 4 green onions, thinly sliced
- 4 slices cheddar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 tsp sesame seeds

From the pantry: ¾ cup flour, ¼ cup cornstarch, 1 cup water, ½ tsp salt, 2 tbsp oil, sriracha to serve (optional)

## Steps

1. Whisk the flour, cornstarch, salt and water into a thin, smooth batter. In another bowl, beat the eggs with the green onions and a pinch of salt. Stir the soy, honey and 2 tsp water together for the sauce.
2. Heat a 10-inch nonstick skillet over medium and brush with a little oil. Pour in ¼ cup batter and swirl to coat the pan. Cook until set and pale golden underneath, about 2 minutes.
3. Lift the crepe with a spatula, pour a quarter of the egg mixture into the pan, and lay the crepe right back on top so it sticks to the egg. Cook until the egg is set, about 1 minute.
4. Flip so the egg side is up, lay a slice of cheese on top and roll the crepe up like a cigar. Cook the roll 30 seconds per side so the cheese melts.
5. Slice each roll into 5 pieces, drizzle with the sweet soy, sprinkle with sesame seeds and add sriracha if you like. Repeat with the rest.

**Tip:** Taiwanese shops often add corn, ham, tuna or bacon with the cheese, so try whatever you have. Thick Taiwanese soy paste is the classic drizzle.

Adapted from [Cooks Without Borders (Frankie Gaw)](https://cookswithoutborders.com/dan-bing).

## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer up to 1 months. Freeze plain cooked crepes between sheets of parchment and add fresh egg when you cook them.
