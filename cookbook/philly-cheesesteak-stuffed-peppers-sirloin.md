# Philly Cheesesteak Stuffed Peppers with Provolone

**Dinner** · American comfort · 45 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Roasted green pepper halves stuffed with thin-sliced sirloin, caramelized onions and mushrooms in a savory Worcestershire pan sauce, tucked under and over with melting provolone.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb top sirloin, sliced paper-thin
- 4 large green bell peppers, halved and seeded
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 8 oz mushrooms, chopped
- 8 slices provolone, halved

From the pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp onion powder, cooking spray, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Mist the pepper halves with cooking spray, season with salt and roast cut-side up until starting to soften, 15 minutes.
2. Heat half the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the onion and mushrooms with a pinch of salt until browned and soft, 8 to 10 minutes. Remove.
3. Add the rest of the oil and sear the steak in batches until just browned, 1 to 2 minutes. Return the vegetables, add the Worcestershire, garlic powder, onion powder and pepper and toss.
4. Lay a half slice of provolone inside each pepper, pack in the steak filling and top with another half slice.
5. Bake until the cheese melts, 5 to 8 minutes, then broil 1 to 2 minutes until golden.

**Tip:** Freeze the sirloin 30 minutes before slicing; it's the easiest way to get the shaved, deli-thin strips a cheesesteak needs.

Adapted from [USA Rice](https://www.usarice.com/thinkrice/recipes/philly-cheesesteak-stuffed-peppers).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 2 months. Reheat in a 375°F oven 12 to 15 minutes or microwave 2 minutes.
