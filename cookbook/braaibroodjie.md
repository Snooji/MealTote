# Braaibroodjies (Chutney, Cheddar & Tomato Toasties)

**Lunch** · South African · 20 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> South Africa's braai-side grilled cheese: sharp cheddar, tomato, raw onion and sweet-tangy chutney between buttery bread, toasted until crisp, with a simple green salad.

## Ingredients

- 8 slices sourdough or white bread
- 8 oz sharp cheddar, shredded
- 2 Roma tomatoes, thinly sliced
- ½ onion, very thinly sliced
- ¼ cup mango chutney
- 4 tbsp softened butter
- 5 oz spring mix

From the pantry: 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp vinegar, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Butter one side of each slice of bread. Turn 4 slices butter-side down and spread with chutney.
2. Top the chutney slices with half the cheese, the tomato and onion, a pinch of salt and pepper, then the rest of the cheese. Close with the remaining slices, butter side up.
3. Toast in a large skillet over medium-low, pressing gently with a spatula, until deep golden and the cheese melts, 3 to 4 minutes per side. On the grill, use a hinged grid over medium coals.
4. Toss the greens with the oil, vinegar and salt, cut the toasties in half and serve hot.

**Tip:** Mrs. Ball's peach chutney is the iconic choice and is sold at many World Market stores and online; any sweet fruit chutney works.

Adapted from [Tara's Multicultural Table](https://tarasmulticulturaltable.com/braaibroodjie-south-african-barbeque-sandwich/).

## Storing

Fridge 1 days · freezer not recommended. Best fresh. Re-crisp in a 375°F oven for 5 minutes.
