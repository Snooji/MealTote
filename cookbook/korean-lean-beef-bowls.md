# Korean Gochujang Beef Bowls with Edamame & Cucumber

**Lunch** · Korean · 25 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Lean ground beef glazed in a sweet, garlicky gochujang-soy sauce over rice and cauliflower rice, with edamame, cucumber, carrots and sesame. Ready in under half an hour.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb 93% lean ground beef
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1½ tbsp grated ginger
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- ½ bunch green onions, sliced
- ½ cup rice
- ½ bag frozen riced cauliflower
- 1 cup shelled edamame
- ½ English cucumber, sliced
- 1 carrot, shredded
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds

From the pantry: 2 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp rice vinegar

## Steps

1. Cook the rice. Warm the cauliflower rice and edamame in the microwave or a pan, then fluff them with the rice.
2. Brown the beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up, until crisp in spots, 7 to 8 minutes. Pour off any fat.
3. Add the garlic, ginger and the white parts of the green onions and cook 1 minute.
4. Whisk the soy sauce, gochujang, brown sugar, vinegar and sesame oil, pour it over the beef and simmer until glossy and absorbed, 2 to 3 minutes.
5. Serve the beef over the rice mix with edamame, cucumber, carrot, green onion tops and sesame seeds.

**Tip:** Let the beef sit undisturbed for a minute or two at a time while browning; crispy bits soak up the glaze best.

Adapted from [Spend With Pennies](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/korean-beef-bowl/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Microwave 2 minutes. Add the cucumber and carrot cold after reheating.
