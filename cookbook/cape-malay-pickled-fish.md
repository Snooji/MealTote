# Cape Malay Pickled Fish with Curried Onions

**Lunch** · South African · 45 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Cape Town's Easter favorite: golden fried fish layered with sweet-sour onions in a turmeric-yellow, gently curried vinegar, chilled overnight and eaten with buttered bread.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb cod, hake or other firm white fish, in 3-inch pieces
- 3 onions, sliced into rings
- 2 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- crusty bread, sliced, for serving

From the pantry: 1 cup vinegar, ½ cup sugar, ⅓ cup flour, ¼ cup oil, 2 tbsp curry powder, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground coriander, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 6 allspice berries, 3 bay leaves, salt

## Steps

1. Season the fish with salt and 1 tsp curry powder, then dust in flour. Fry in 3 tbsp oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high until golden and just cooked, 3 minutes per side. Lay in a glass dish.
2. In a pot, cook the onions in the remaining oil over medium for 3 minutes so they soften but keep some crunch. Add the garlic, ginger, the rest of the curry powder, turmeric, coriander and cumin seeds for 1 minute.
3. Add the vinegar, ¾ cup water, sugar, allspice, bay leaves and 1 tsp salt. Simmer 5 minutes and taste: it should be sharply sweet-sour.
4. Pour the hot onions and liquid over the fish so it is covered. Cool, then cover and refrigerate at least 24 hours (2 to 3 days is better).
5. Serve cold or at room temperature with buttered bread.

**Tip:** Cape cooks use snoek, yellowtail or kingklip; any firm white fish that won't flake apart works. Many families buy ready-made 'pickled fish masala' for the spice.

Adapted from [Tantalise My Taste Buds](https://www.tantalisemytastebuds.com/cape-malay-pickled-fish/).

## Storing

Fridge 5 days · freezer not recommended. Keep fish covered in the pickle in a glass dish. Eat cold or at room temperature. No reheating needed.
