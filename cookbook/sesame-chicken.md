# Crispy Sesame Chicken

**Dinner** · Chinese · 35 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Takeout-style crispy chicken in a glossy, sweet-savory sesame glaze with steamed broccoli and rice.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb boneless thighs, in 1-inch pieces
- 1 egg
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup honey
- 2 tbsp hoisin
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 lb broccoli
- 1½ cups rice
- green onions

From the pantry: ¾ cup cornstarch, ⅓ cup oil, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sesame oil, salt

## Steps

1. Start the rice. Whisk the soy, honey, hoisin, vinegar, garlic, sesame oil, 1 tbsp cornstarch and ¼ cup water for the sauce.
2. Toss the chicken with the egg and a pinch of salt, then with the rest of the cornstarch until coated.
3. Shallow-fry in batches in the oil over medium-high until golden and crisp, 5 to 6 minutes. Drain on a rack. Steam the broccoli.
4. Pour off the oil, add the sauce to the pan and simmer, stirring, until thick and shiny, 1 to 2 minutes.
5. Toss the chicken in the sauce, sprinkle with sesame seeds and green onions, and serve over rice with broccoli.

**Tip:** Fry the chicken a second time for 1 minute just before saucing if you want it extra crunchy.

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 2 months. Re-crisp in a 400°F oven or air fryer for 6 to 8 minutes.
