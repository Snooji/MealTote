# Baghrir (Thousand-Hole Semolina Pancakes) with Honey Butter

**Breakfast** · Moroccan · 50 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Spongy, lacy Moroccan pancakes cooked on one side only so their tops bloom with tiny holes, then drenched in warm honey butter and served with oranges.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups fine semolina
- 6 tbsp butter
- ¼ cup honey
- 1 lb navel oranges, segmented

From the pantry: ½ cup flour, 1 tsp instant yeast, 2 tsp baking powder, 2 tsp sugar, ½ tsp salt

## Steps

1. Blend the semolina, flour, yeast, baking powder, sugar, salt and 2 cups warm water for 1 minute until completely smooth.
2. Cover and let the batter rest 30 minutes until bubbly. It should be like heavy cream; add a splash of water if it is thicker.
3. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium-low, wipe with a little butter, and pour in ¼ cup batter. Cook without flipping until the top is covered in holes and looks dry, 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Move to a towel-lined plate in a single layer (they stick if stacked hot). Repeat with the rest.
5. Melt the remaining butter with the honey and spoon over the pancakes. Serve with oranges.

**Tip:** If no holes form, the batter needs more rest or the pan is too hot; lower the heat a notch and wait 10 minutes.

Adapted from [Salima's Kitchen](https://salimaskitchen.com/baghrir/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 2 months. Freeze between parchment. Warm in a skillet or microwave 20 seconds, then add honey butter.
