# Philly Cheesesteak Egg-White Omelets

**Breakfast** · American comfort · 35 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Everything you love about a cheesesteak folded into a big, fluffy egg-white omelet: seared shaved steak, sweet caramelized onions and peppers, and a blanket of melty provolone.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz shaved steak
- 4 cups (one 32-oz carton) liquid egg whites
- 1 green and 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 slices provolone, torn

From the pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, cooking spray

## Steps

1. Heat 2 tsp of the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the onion and peppers with a pinch of salt, stirring now and then, until soft and browned, 8 to 10 minutes. Add the garlic for 30 seconds and scrape into a bowl.
2. Add the remaining oil and spread the shaved steak in the pan. Season with salt and pepper and sear without moving for 2 minutes, then toss and chop it with your spatula until just cooked, 1 to 2 minutes more. Stir in the Worcestershire and the vegetables. Keep warm.
3. Coat an 8- or 10-inch nonstick skillet with cooking spray and set over medium-low heat. Add 1 cup egg whites with a pinch of salt and cook, lifting the edges to let the raw egg run under, until nearly set, 3 to 4 minutes.
4. Spoon a quarter of the steak filling over one half, top with a slice of provolone, cover for 1 minute to melt, then fold and slide onto a plate.
5. Repeat with the rest of the egg whites, filling and cheese.

**Tip:** If you can't find shaved steak, freeze a sirloin for 30 minutes and slice it paper-thin across the grain; it cooks in seconds and stays tender.

Adapted from [Spiced](https://spicedblog.com/philly-cheesesteak-omelet/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer not recommended. Refrigerate the steak filling separately for up to 4 days and make the omelets fresh.
