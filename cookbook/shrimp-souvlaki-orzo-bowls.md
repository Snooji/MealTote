# Shrimp Souvlaki Bowls with Lemon Orzo & Tzatziki

**Lunch** · Greek · 30 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Lemon-oregano shrimp seared hot and fast, piled over lemony orzo with a chopped cucumber-tomato salad, olives, feta and a thick homemade yogurt tzatziki. Bright, fresh and full of protein.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb large shrimp, peeled
- 1 large lemon, zested and juiced
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 1⅓ cups orzo
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 English cucumber, half grated, half diced
- 1 pint grape tomatoes, halved
- 1 romaine heart, shredded
- ¼ cup Kalamata olives
- ½ cup crumbled feta
- ¼ cup chopped parsley

From the pantry: 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp dried dill, ½ tsp smoked paprika, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Boil the orzo in salted water until tender, 8 to 9 minutes. Drain and toss with half the lemon juice, the zest and a pinch of salt.
2. Toss the shrimp with 1 tbsp olive oil, 2 cloves garlic, the oregano, paprika, ½ tsp salt and pepper.
3. Squeeze the grated cucumber dry and stir it into the yogurt with the remaining garlic, the dill, half the parsley, a squeeze of lemon and salt for tzatziki.
4. Heat the remaining oil in a large skillet over high heat and sear the shrimp in a single layer until pink and just curled, 1½ to 2 minutes per side.
5. Build bowls with orzo, romaine, diced cucumber, tomatoes and olives. Top with shrimp, feta, a big spoon of tzatziki, the rest of the parsley and the last of the lemon juice.

**Tip:** Sear shrimp in two batches if your pan is small; crowded shrimp steam and turn rubbery instead of browning.

Adapted from [Slender Kitchen](https://www.slenderkitchen.com/recipe/mediterranean-shrimp-bowls).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer not recommended. Eat cold like a salad, or warm the shrimp and orzo 60 seconds before adding the toppings.
