# Pepperoni Pizza Grilled Cheese

> A grilled cheese crossed with a pepperoni pizza: buttery toasted sourdough, gooey mozzarella, pepperoni and a little sauce inside, with warm marinara for dunking.

## Ingredients

- 8 slices sourdough
- 1½ cups shredded mozzarella
- 3 oz sliced pepperoni
- 1 cup marinara, divided
- 3 tbsp grated Parmesan
- 3 tbsp softened butter
- a few basil leaves, torn

From the pantry: ½ tsp dried oregano, ¼ tsp garlic powder

## Steps

1. Mix the softened butter with the garlic powder and spread it on one side of each bread slice.
2. Lay 4 slices butter side down. Spread each with 1 tbsp marinara, then layer half the mozzarella, the pepperoni, Parmesan, oregano, basil and the rest of the mozzarella. Top with the remaining bread, butter side up.
3. Cook in a large skillet over medium-low heat until deep golden underneath, 3 to 4 minutes. Flip, press gently with a spatula and cook until the cheese is fully melted, 3 to 4 minutes more.
4. Warm the remaining marinara in a small pan or the microwave.
5. Cut the sandwiches in half and serve with the warm sauce for dipping.

**Tip:** Keep the heat at medium-low. The bread browns slowly while the thick layer of cheese has time to melt all the way through.

Adapted from [Spend With Pennies](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/pizza-grilled-cheese/).

## Storing

Fridge 1 days · freezer not recommended. Best fresh. Reheat leftovers in a skillet or air fryer to re-crisp.
