# Garlic-Ginger Ground Beef & Broccoli Rice Bowls

**Dinner** · Chinese & Cantonese · 30 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Takeout-style beef and broccoli made with lean ground beef, so it's on the table in 30 minutes: crispy browned beef and tender-crisp broccoli in a glossy garlic-ginger soy sauce over rice.

## Ingredients

- 2½ lb 93% lean ground beef
- 1½ lb broccoli, in small florets
- ½ onion, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1½ tbsp grated ginger
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- ⅔ cup beef broth
- ¾ cup rice
- 3 green onions, sliced
- 1½ tsp sesame seeds

From the pantry: 1 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp sriracha, salt

## Steps

1. Cook the rice. Whisk the soy sauce, broth, brown sugar, cornstarch, sesame oil and sriracha for the sauce.
2. Brown the beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up and letting it sit so it crisps in spots, 8 to 10 minutes. Pour off any fat.
3. Add the onion, garlic and ginger and cook 2 minutes, until fragrant.
4. Add the broccoli and ¼ cup water, cover and steam until bright green and just tender, 3 to 4 minutes.
5. Stir the sauce, pour it in and simmer, tossing, until thick and glossy, 1 to 2 minutes. Serve over rice with green onions and sesame seeds.

**Tip:** Cut the broccoli small so it cooks in the time it takes the sauce to thicken; big florets mean overcooked beef.

Adapted from [I Wash You Dry](https://iwashyoudry.com/ground-beef-and-broccoli/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Microwave 2 minutes, stirring once. A splash of water loosens the sauce.
