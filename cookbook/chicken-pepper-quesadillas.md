# Cheesy Chicken & Pepper Quesadillas

**Lunch** · Tex-Mex · 25 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Crisp, golden flour tortillas stuffed with taco-seasoned chicken, sautéed peppers and onions and a heap of melted cheese, served with salsa and sour cream.

## Ingredients

- 4 large flour tortillas
- 1½ cups shredded rotisserie chicken
- 1 bell pepper, thinly sliced
- ½ onion, thinly sliced
- ½ packet taco seasoning
- 2 cups shredded Mexican cheese
- ¼ cup sour cream
- ½ cup salsa

From the pantry: 2 tbsp oil

## Steps

1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the onion and pepper until soft and lightly charred, 4 to 5 minutes.
2. Add the chicken, taco seasoning and ¼ cup water and simmer, stirring, until the liquid is mostly gone, 2 to 3 minutes. Transfer to a bowl and wipe out the pan.
3. Sprinkle ¼ cup cheese over half of each tortilla, add a quarter of the chicken mixture, then another ¼ cup cheese. Fold the tortillas over.
4. Brush the skillet with a little oil and cook 2 quesadillas at a time over medium-low heat until golden and the cheese has melted, 3 to 4 minutes per side.
5. Let them rest 2 minutes, cut each into 3 wedges and serve with salsa and sour cream.

**Tip:** Putting cheese on both sides of the filling glues the quesadilla together so nothing falls out when you flip or cut it.

Adapted from [Spend With Pennies](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/chicken-quesadillas/).

## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer up to 2 months. Freeze cooked, cooled quesadillas between parchment. Reheat in a dry skillet over medium-low for 4 to 5 minutes per side.
