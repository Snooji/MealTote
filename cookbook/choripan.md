# Choripán with Chimichurri & Salsa Criolla

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Lunch** · Argentinian · 30 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Argentina's favorite asado snack: juicy sausages split butterfly-style, charred on the grill and tucked into crusty rolls with garlicky chimichurri and a fresh tomato-pepper salsa.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fresh sausage links (4 to 5)
- 4 crusty rolls
- 1 bunch parsley, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
- ½ red onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced

From the pantry: ⅓ cup olive oil, 3 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tsp dried oregano, ½ tsp red pepper flakes, ½ tsp smoked paprika, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. For the chimichurri, mix the parsley, garlic, half the oregano, the pepper flakes, paprika, ¼ cup oil, 2 tbsp vinegar, salt and pepper. Let it sit while you cook.
2. For the salsa criolla, toss the tomatoes, onion and bell pepper with the rest of the oil, vinegar and oregano and a pinch of salt.
3. Grill the sausages over medium heat, or cook in a covered skillet with a splash of water, turning, until 160°F inside, 12 to 15 minutes.
4. Slice each sausage lengthwise most of the way through, open it flat, and grill cut side down until charred, 1 to 2 minutes. Toast the split rolls.
5. Spread chimichurri inside each roll, add a sausage, and top with salsa criolla and more chimichurri.

**Tip:** Look for Argentine-style chorizo at Latin markets: it's a fresh, mild pork-and-beef sausage, not the crumbly Mexican kind. Mild Italian or homemade turkey sausage are good stand-ins.

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 2 months. Chimichurri keeps a week in the fridge; reheat sausages in a skillet and build sandwiches fresh.
