# Costa Rican Gallo Pinto with Fried Eggs

**Breakfast** · Salvadoran & Central American · 30 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Day-old rice and black beans fried together with onion, sweet pepper and lots of cilantro until speckled and savory, served with fried eggs and a spoonful of sour cream.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups rice, cooked and cooled (about 4 cups cooked)
- 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, with ½ cup of their liquid
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 1 red bell pepper, finely diced
- ½ bunch cilantro, chopped
- 4 eggs
- ½ cup sour cream
- 2 limes, in wedges

From the pantry: 3 tbsp oil, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp hot sauce, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Cook the rice a day ahead if you can and chill it; cold rice fries without turning mushy.
2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium. Cook the onion and pepper with a pinch of salt until soft, 5 to 6 minutes. Add the cumin for 30 seconds.
3. Add the beans with ½ cup of their liquid and simmer until the liquid reduces by half, 3 to 4 minutes.
4. Add the rice and fold everything together, pressing gently, until the rice is hot and stained dark by the beans, 5 to 6 minutes. Stir in the hot sauce, most of the cilantro, and salt and pepper to taste.
5. Fry the eggs in the remaining oil in a second pan until the whites are set, 3 minutes. Serve the gallo pinto with an egg, sour cream, cilantro and lime wedges.

**Tip:** If you can find Salsa Lizano in the Latin aisle, a few spoonfuls stirred in at the end is the authentic Costa Rican finish.

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Store without eggs. Reheat in a skillet with a splash of water until hot, 5 minutes.
