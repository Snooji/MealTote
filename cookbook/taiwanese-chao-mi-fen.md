# Taiwanese Fried Rice Vermicelli with Shrimp & Shiitake (Chao Mi Fen)

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Lunch** · Taiwanese · 35 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Every Taiwanese grandma's party noodle: thin rice vermicelli simmered in a savory broth with cabbage, shiitakes and carrot until it absorbs every drop, then finished with shrimp and crispy fried shallots.

## Ingredients

- 10½ oz rice vermicelli
- ¾ lb raw peeled shrimp
- 5 oz shiitake mushrooms, sliced
- ½ head green cabbage (1 lb), thinly sliced
- 1 carrot, in matchsticks
- 2 celery ribs, thinly sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup oyster sauce
- 2½ cups chicken broth
- ⅓ cup fried shallots
- cilantro to serve

From the pantry: 3 tbsp oil, 1 tsp white pepper, ½ tsp sugar

## Steps

1. Soak the vermicelli in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain. Stir the soy, oyster sauce, white pepper, sugar and broth together.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large wok or deep skillet over medium-high. Cook the shrimp until just pink, about 2 minutes, and move to a plate.
3. Add the remaining oil and the garlic and shiitakes and stir-fry until the mushrooms soften, 2 to 3 minutes. Add the cabbage, carrot and celery and cook until the cabbage wilts, 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Pour in the broth mixture and bring to a simmer. Add the noodles and loosen them into the liquid with chopsticks or tongs.
5. Turn the heat to low, cover and cook, lifting and tossing every few minutes, until the noodles are tender and have absorbed the liquid, about 10 minutes. Add a splash of water if they are still firm.
6. Toss in the shrimp and half the fried shallots, then pile onto a platter and top with the rest of the shallots and the cilantro.

**Tip:** The noodles should be soft and seasoned all the way through, not wet. Keep tossing with chopsticks instead of stirring so the strands stay long. Thin strips of pork and dried shrimp are also traditional.

Adapted from [Elle Gourmet (Tiffy Chen)](https://ellegourmet.ca/taiwanese-stir-fry-noodles-recipe/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer not recommended. Reheat in a skillet with a few spoonfuls of water, covered, until steaming.
