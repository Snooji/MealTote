# Greek Lemon Chicken & Potatoes

**Dinner** · Greek · 60 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Crispy-skinned chicken thighs roasted over potato wedges that soak up a bright lemon, garlic and oregano broth.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb bone-in chicken thighs (or boneless)
- 2 lb potatoes, in wedges
- 3 lemons
- 6 garlic cloves
- ¾ cup broth
- olives (optional)
- feta, to serve
- parsley

From the pantry: ¼ cup olive oil, 1 tbsp dried oregano, 1½ tsp salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Whisk the oil, juice of 2 lemons, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper.
2. Toss the potatoes with half the mixture in a roasting pan and pour in the broth.
3. Coat the chicken with the rest and nestle on top of the potatoes, skin-side up.
4. Roast 45 to 50 minutes, basting once, until the chicken is golden and the potatoes are tender and browned at the edges.
5. Scatter with olives, feta, parsley and lemon wedges.

**Tip:** If the pan dries out, add a splash more broth. You want a little lemony sauce at the end.


## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Reheat at 375°F for 15 minutes to re-crisp.
