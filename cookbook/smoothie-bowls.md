# Berry Banana Smoothie Bowls

> Thick, spoonable frozen berry and banana smoothie bowls blended with Greek yogurt and a handful of spinach, topped with granola, banana and peanut butter.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups frozen mixed berries
- 3 bananas (2 frozen)
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 handful spinach
- ¼ cup milk
- granola
- peanut butter
- chia seeds

From the pantry: Honey (optional)

## Steps

1. Blend the frozen berries, 2 frozen bananas, yogurt, spinach and just enough milk to get it moving. Keep it thick enough to hold a spoon upright.
2. Scoop into 2 bowls.
3. Slice the remaining banana.
4. Top with rows of granola, banana, a drizzle of peanut butter and chia seeds.
5. Eat right away.

**Tip:** Peel and freeze ripe bananas in chunks so you always have smoothie bowls ready.

## Storing

Fridge 0 days · freezer up to 3 months. Freeze pre-portioned smoothie packs of fruit, spinach and yogurt cubes. Blend fresh.
