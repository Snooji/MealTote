# Kotlety (Russian Turkey & Pork Patties) with Mash & Cucumber Salad

**Dinner** · Russian & Ukrainian · 55 min · serves 5 · skill 2/3

> Juicy, tender pan-fried patties of ground turkey and pork with grated onion and herbs: the weeknight dinner of every Russian home, with buttery mashed potatoes and a dill-cucumber salad.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 lb ground pork
- 1 onion, grated
- 1 egg
- ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
- 2½ lb russet potatoes
- ⅔ cup milk
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 English cucumber, sliced
- ¼ cup sour cream
- 2 tbsp chopped dill

From the pantry: 3 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp mayonnaise, 1¼ tsp salt, ½ tsp garlic powder, ¼ tsp pepper

## Steps

1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender, 15 to 18 minutes. Drain and mash with the butter, warm milk and salt to taste; keep warm.
2. Mix the turkey, pork, grated onion with its juice, egg, panko, parsley, mayonnaise, 1 tsp salt, garlic powder and pepper until evenly combined. Shape into about 15 oval patties with wet hands.
3. Heat the oven to 350°F. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high and brown the patties in batches, 3 minutes per side, without pressing them flat.
4. Move the patties to a baking dish, cover with foil and bake until 165°F inside, about 10 minutes.
5. Toss the cucumber with the sour cream, dill and a pinch of salt. Serve the kotlety with the mash and salad.

**Tip:** The grated onion juice is what keeps kotlety juicy, so grate it straight into the bowl. All-turkey works too; add an extra tablespoon of mayo or a little milk-soaked bread to keep them tender.

Adapted from [Natasha's Kitchen](https://natashaskitchen.com/russian-katleti-turkey-and-pork-patties/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Refrigerate or freeze the patties. Reheat covered at 350°F for 15 minutes, or in a covered skillet with a splash of water.
