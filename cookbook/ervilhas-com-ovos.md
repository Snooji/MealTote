# Ervilhas com Ovos (Portuguese Peas with Poached Eggs & Linguiça)

**Breakfast** · Portuguese · 40 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> A thrifty Portuguese home classic: sweet peas stewed with onion, garlic and smoky sausage, with eggs poached right on top and crusty bread for dunking into the yolks.

## Ingredients

- 24 oz frozen peas
- 3 oz linguiça or chouriço, sliced
- 6 eggs
- 1 onion, chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, peeled
- ½ bunch cilantro, chopped
- crusty bread, for serving

From the pantry: ¼ cup olive oil, 2 cups water, 1 bay leaf, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper

## Steps

1. Warm the olive oil in a wide, deep skillet over medium-low. Add the onion, whole garlic cloves, sausage and bay leaf and cook, stirring now and then, until the onion is golden, 6 to 7 minutes.
2. Add the peas, salt and pepper, pour in the water and bring to a boil. Cover and simmer over medium-low until the peas are very tender and the broth has thickened slightly, 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Remove the bay leaf and stir in most of the cilantro. Make 6 wells in the peas and crack an egg into each.
4. Cover and cook over low heat until the whites are set and the yolks are still soft, 6 to 8 minutes. Sprinkle with salt and the rest of the cilantro and serve straight from the pan with bread.

**Tip:** Smoked paprika in the oil adds the flavor of Portuguese chouriço if your sausage is mild. Leftover peas (without eggs) make a great base for poaching fresh eggs the next day.

Adapted from [Food From Portugal](https://www.foodfromportugal.com/recipes/peas-poached-eggs-chourico/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer not recommended. Store the peas without eggs; reheat and poach fresh eggs in them.
