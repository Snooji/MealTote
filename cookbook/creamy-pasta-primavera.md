# Creamy Pasta Primavera with Parmesan

**Dinner** · Italian · 30 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Penne loaded with tender-crisp zucchini, asparagus, peppers, peas and sweet tomatoes in a light, garlicky Parmesan cream sauce. A colorful meatless night everyone likes.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz penne
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- ½ lb asparagus, in 2-inch pieces
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- ¾ cup frozen peas
- 1 cup grape tomatoes, halved
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- a handful of basil, torn

From the pantry: 1 tbsp cornstarch, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp pepper, pinch of red pepper flakes

## Steps

1. Cook the penne in salted water until al dente; drain.
2. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat and cook the garlic until fragrant, 1 minute. Add the zucchini, asparagus and pepper and cook 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the broth and simmer until the vegetables are tender-crisp, 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Stir in the cream, peas and tomatoes and simmer 2 minutes. Mix the cornstarch with 1 tbsp water, whisk it in and simmer until the sauce thickens, about 1 minute.
5. Stir in the Parmesan, salt, pepper and pepper flakes, then toss with the pasta. Top with basil and more Parmesan.

**Tip:** Swap in whatever vegetables are in season or in your crisper, such as broccoli, mushrooms or spinach. Just cut them about the same size so they cook evenly.

Adapted from [Spend With Pennies](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/pasta-primavera/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer not recommended. Refrigerate. Reheat gently in a skillet with a splash of broth or milk.
