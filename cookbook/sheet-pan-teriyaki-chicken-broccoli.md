# Sheet-Pan Teriyaki Chicken & Broccoli with Rice

**Dinner** · Japanese · 40 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Chicken and broccoli roasted on one pan and lacquered with a homemade honey-garlic teriyaki glaze, finished with sesame and green onions over fluffy rice. Better than takeout and easy to portion.

## Ingredients

- 2¼ lb boneless chicken breasts, in 1-inch pieces
- 1½ lb broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, in chunks
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- 3 tbsp honey
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1 cup rice
- 1½ tsp sesame seeds
- 3 green onions, sliced

From the pantry: 1 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp sesame oil, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, salt

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Cook the rice.
2. Simmer the soy sauce, honey, vinegar, garlic, ginger, sesame oil and ⅓ cup water in a small pot. Whisk the cornstarch with 2 tbsp water, stir it in and cook until thick, about 1 minute.
3. Toss the broccoli and pepper with the oil and a pinch of salt on one side of a large sheet pan. Toss the chicken with ⅓ of the teriyaki and spread it on the other side.
4. Roast 12 minutes, brush the chicken with another ⅓ of the sauce and roast until 165°F inside and the broccoli is charred at the tips, 6 to 8 minutes more.
5. Toss everything with the last of the sauce and serve over rice with sesame seeds and green onions.

**Tip:** Hold back some sauce until after roasting; glazing at the end keeps the broccoli crisp instead of soggy.

Adapted from [Savory Sweet Spoon](https://www.savorysweetspoon.com/sheet-pan-teriyaki-chicken/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Microwave 2 minutes with a teaspoon of water over the rice.
