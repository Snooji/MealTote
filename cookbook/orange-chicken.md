# Better-Than-Takeout Orange Chicken

**Dinner** · Chinese & Cantonese · 35 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Crispy cornstarch-coated chicken tossed in a sticky, tangy sauce made with fresh orange juice and zest, served with rice and steamed broccoli.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb boneless chicken thighs, in 1-inch pieces
- 1 egg
- 2 oranges (zest and ¾ cup juice)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- ¼ cup honey
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 1-inch ginger, grated
- 1½ cups jasmine rice
- 1 lb broccoli
- green onions
- sesame seeds

From the pantry: ¾ cup cornstarch, ½ cup oil, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, red pepper flakes, salt

## Steps

1. Start the rice. Whisk the orange zest and juice, soy, honey, vinegar, garlic, ginger, pepper flakes and 1 tbsp cornstarch for the sauce.
2. Toss the chicken with the egg and a pinch of salt, then with the remaining cornstarch until every piece is coated.
3. Shallow-fry in batches in ¼ inch hot oil until deep golden and crisp, 5 to 6 minutes. Drain. Steam the broccoli.
4. Wipe out the pan and simmer the sauce, stirring, until thick and glossy, 2 minutes.
5. Toss the chicken in the sauce right before serving. Top with green onions and sesame seeds.

**Tip:** Toss at the last second. Crispy chicken goes soft if it sits in sauce.


## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 2 months. Reheat in a 400°F oven or air fryer for 6 to 8 minutes to re-crisp.
