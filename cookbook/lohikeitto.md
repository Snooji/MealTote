# Lohikeitto (Finnish Creamy Salmon & Dill Soup)

**Dinner** · Scandinavian · 45 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Finland's beloved salmon soup: tender potatoes, onion and carrot in a light, creamy broth with big chunks of gently poached salmon and a fistful of fresh dill, served with dark rye bread.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ lb skinless salmon, in 1-inch cubes
- 1½ lb potatoes, in ¾-inch cubes
- 1 large onion or 1 leek, chopped
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 3 cups fish stock or vegetable broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 bunch dill, chopped
- 2 tbsp butter
- dark rye bread, to serve

From the pantry: 1 bay leaf, 6 allspice berries or ¼ tsp ground allspice, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium and cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the potatoes, carrots, broth, bay leaf, allspice and 1 tsp salt. Bring to a boil, then simmer until the potatoes are just tender, 12 to 15 minutes.
3. Pour in the cream and bring back to a gentle simmer; do not boil hard.
4. Slide in the salmon and half the dill, then cook on low, without stirring much, until the salmon is just opaque, 4 to 5 minutes.
5. Season with salt and pepper, scatter with the remaining dill and serve with buttered rye bread.

**Tip:** Add the salmon at the very end and nudge rather than stir so the chunks stay whole. Finnish cooks use leeks; use the white and light green parts, well rinsed.

Adapted from [196 flavors](https://www.196flavors.com/finland-lohikeitto/).

## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer not recommended. Reheat gently over low heat just until hot so the salmon doesn't overcook.
