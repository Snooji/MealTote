# Lightened-Up Chicken Marsala with Mushrooms & Spaghetti

**Dinner** · Italian · 40 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Golden chicken cutlets in a rich, savory Marsala and mushroom pan sauce with just a splash of cream, with zucchini and peppers cooked in the same skillet and a small twirl of spaghetti.

## Ingredients

- 2¼ lb boneless chicken breasts, sliced into thin cutlets
- 16 oz sliced cremini mushrooms
- ½ onion, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ cup chicken broth
- 2 tbsp heavy cream
- 2 zucchini, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 4 oz spaghetti
- ¼ cup chopped parsley

From the pantry: 2 tbsp flour, for dredging, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp butter, ⅓ cup dry Marsala wine, ½ tsp dried thyme, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water. Season the cutlets with salt and pepper and dredge lightly in flour.
2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and brown the chicken in batches, 3 minutes per side, until 165°F inside. Move to a plate.
3. Melt the butter in the pan and cook the zucchini and pepper 3 minutes; push them aside, add the mushrooms and onion and cook until the mushrooms are browned, 6 to 7 minutes. Add the garlic and thyme for 30 seconds.
4. Pour in the Marsala and scrape up the browned bits, then add the broth and simmer until reduced by half, 3 minutes. Stir in the cream.
5. Return the chicken to the sauce for 2 minutes, then serve with the spaghetti and parsley.

**Tip:** Use dry Marsala, not sweet; if you can't find it, dry sherry or a dry white wine works in a pinch.

Adapted from [The Girl on Bloor](https://thegirlonbloor.com/skinny-easy-chicken-marsala-skillet/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 2 months. Reheat covered at 70% power, 2 minutes, with a spoon of broth.
