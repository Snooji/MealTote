# Sunday Pot Roast with Carrots, Potatoes & Gravy

**Dinner** · American comfort · 240 min · serves 6 · skill 2/3

> Fall-apart chuck roast braised low and slow with onion, garlic and herbs, with carrots and potatoes that soak up all the beefy juices and a silky pan gravy.

## Ingredients

- 3 lb boneless chuck roast
- 2 onions, in wedges
- 5 garlic cloves, smashed
- 3 cups beef broth
- 1½ lb baby potatoes
- 1 lb carrots, in 2-inch pieces
- 2 ribs celery, in 2-inch pieces
- 2 tbsp butter
- parsley, to serve

From the pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 2 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp dried thyme, ½ tsp dried rosemary, 2 bay leaves, 2 tbsp flour, 2 tsp salt, 1 tsp pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 300°F. Pat the roast dry and season all over with salt and pepper. Sear in the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat until deeply browned, 4 to 5 minutes per side. Set aside.
2. Add the onions and cook 3 minutes, then stir in the garlic and tomato paste for 1 minute. Pour in the broth and Worcestershire and scrape up the browned bits. Add the thyme, rosemary and bay leaves.
3. Return the roast, bring to a simmer, cover and braise in the oven for 2 hours.
4. Add the potatoes, carrots and celery around the roast, cover and cook until the beef pulls apart easily with a fork and the vegetables are tender, 1½ to 2 hours more.
5. Move the beef and vegetables to a platter. Mash the butter and flour into a paste, whisk it into the simmering pan juices and cook until thickened into gravy, 2 to 3 minutes. Serve the beef in chunks with the vegetables and gravy, scattered with parsley.

**Tip:** If the beef is still tough, it isn't done. Chuck needs time to break down, so give it another 20 to 30 minutes and check again.

Adapted from [Spend With Pennies](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/perfect-pot-roast/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Freeze beef and gravy together. Reheat covered at 325°F until hot, about 25 minutes.
