# Zupa Ogórkowa (Polish Dill Pickle Soup with Chicken)

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Lunch** · Polish · 50 min · serves 5 · skill 1/3

> A creamy, tangy potato soup brightened with grated dill pickles, their brine and plenty of fresh dill, with tender chicken to make it a meal. Comfort in a bowl, Polish style.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless chicken thighs
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 1 lb potatoes, in ½-inch cubes
- 2 carrots, grated
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 1¼ cups dill pickle chips, grated or finely chopped, plus ½ cup brine
- 3 tbsp butter
- ¾ cup sour cream
- ¼ cup chopped dill
- rye or sourdough bread, to serve

From the pantry: 2 tbsp flour, ¼ tsp ground allspice, 2 bay leaves, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper

## Steps

1. Simmer the chicken thighs in the broth with the bay leaves in a large pot until cooked through, about 20 minutes. Lift them out and shred.
2. Meanwhile melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat and cook the onion, carrot and celery until soft, 6 to 8 minutes. Add the grated pickles and cook 3 minutes more.
3. Add the vegetables and potatoes to the broth with the allspice and simmer until the potatoes are tender, 12 to 15 minutes.
4. Whisk the sour cream with the flour, then whisk in a ladle of hot soup to temper it. Stir it into the pot with the shredded chicken and simmer gently 2 minutes until slightly thickened; do not boil.
5. Season with the pickle brine, salt and pepper to taste (add brine a little at a time), stir in the dill and serve with bread.

**Tip:** Use real fermented or refrigerated deli-style dill pickles if you can; they give the soup its proper sour depth. Classic versions use a pork-rib broth, which works beautifully too.

Adapted from [An Edible Mosaic](https://www.anediblemosaic.com/zupa-ogorkowa-polish-dill-pickle-soup-recipe/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Refrigerate; freeze before adding sour cream if you can. Reheat gently over medium-low without boiling.
