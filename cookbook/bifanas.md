# Bifanas (Portuguese Garlic-Wine Pork Sandwiches)

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Lunch** · Portuguese · 30 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> The sandwich every Portuguese café sells: thin pork cutlets marinated in garlic, white wine and paprika, fried and simmered in their juices, then stuffed into soft rolls with mustard.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ lb boneless pork chops, sliced and pounded thin
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 4 soft rolls
- piri-piri hot sauce, for serving

From the pantry: ¾ cup dry white wine, 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp sweet paprika, 3 bay leaves, yellow mustard, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Layer the pork in a dish, seasoning each layer with salt, pepper, paprika, garlic, torn bay leaves and lemon juice. Pour the wine over, cover and marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours, or overnight.
2. Lift the pork out of the marinade (keep the liquid) and pat dry. Heat the oil in a large skillet over high heat and sear the cutlets in batches until browned, about 1 minute a side.
3. Return all the pork to the pan, pour in the marinade and simmer over medium until the liquid has reduced by half and the pork is tender, 5 to 6 minutes.
4. Warm the rolls, pile in the pork, spoon over some of the pan sauce, and serve with mustard and piri-piri sauce.

**Tip:** Pork tenderloin works too; slice it into ¼-inch medallions and pound flat. Some cafés use lard instead of oil for a richer flavor.

Adapted from [196 flavors](https://www.196flavors.com/portugal-bifana/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 2 months. Store the pork in its sauce; reheat in a skillet and assemble on fresh rolls.
