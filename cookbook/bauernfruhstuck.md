# Bauernfrühstück (German Farmer's Breakfast Skillet)

![Bauernfrühstück (German Farmer's Breakfast Skillet)](../photos/bauernfruhstuck.jpg)

**Breakfast** · German & Austrian · 40 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Crisp golden potatoes, smoky bacon and onion folded into soft-set eggs in one big skillet: the hearty breakfast German farmhouses run on.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb baby gold potatoes
- 6 oz thick-cut bacon, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 8 eggs
- ¼ cup milk
- a handful of chives or green onions, sliced
- dill pickles, to serve

From the pantry: 1 tbsp oil, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp pepper, ½ tsp dried marjoram

## Steps

1. Cover the potatoes with cold salted water, bring to a boil and simmer until a knife slides in easily, 12 to 15 minutes. Drain, cool for a few minutes, then slice ¼ inch thick.
2. In a large nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium heat, cook the bacon until the fat renders and the edges begin to crisp, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the oil and the potato slices in an even layer. Leave them alone until browned underneath, 5 to 6 minutes, then add the onion and cook, turning now and then, until the potatoes are golden and the onion is soft, 6 to 8 minutes more.
4. Whisk the eggs with the milk, marjoram, salt and pepper. Turn the heat to medium-low and pour the eggs evenly over the potatoes.
5. Cover and cook without stirring until the eggs are just set on top, 4 to 6 minutes. Scatter with chives and cut into wedges. Serve with pickles on the side.

**Tip:** Potatoes boiled the night before slice cleaner and brown better, so this is the perfect use for leftover boiled potatoes.

Adapted from [Elle Republic](https://ellerepublic.de/en/potato-and-bacon-skillet/).

## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer not recommended. Refrigerate in a covered container. Reheat in a covered skillet over medium-low for 5 minutes; the eggs firm up a bit.
