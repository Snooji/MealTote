# Chicken Hummus Bowls with Quinoa, Feta & Lemon Yogurt

> Smoky paprika-cumin chicken over a swoosh of hummus and fluffy quinoa with cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, olives and feta, finished with a garlicky lemon yogurt sauce.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless chicken breasts
- ⅔ cup hummus
- ⅔ cup quinoa
- 1 English cucumber, diced
- 1 pint grape tomatoes, halved
- ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
- ¼ cup Kalamata olives
- ½ cup crumbled feta
- 1 lemon
- ⅔ cup plain Greek yogurt

From the pantry: 1 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp onion powder, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Rinse the quinoa and simmer it in 1⅓ cups salted water, covered, until tender, 15 minutes. Fluff and cool.
2. Rub the chicken with the oil, paprika, cumin, onion powder, ½ tsp salt and pepper and squeeze half the lemon over it.
3. Cook the chicken in a skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat, 5 to 6 minutes per side, until 165°F inside. Rest 5 minutes and slice.
4. Stir the yogurt with the garlic powder, the rest of the lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
5. Spread hummus in each bowl and add quinoa, chicken, cucumber, tomatoes, onion, olives and feta. Drizzle with the lemon yogurt.

**Tip:** Toast the rinsed quinoa in the dry pot for 2 minutes before adding water; it tastes nuttier and cooks up fluffier.

Adapted from [SueBee Homemaker](https://suebeehomemaker.com/chicken-hummus-bowls/).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer not recommended. Eat cold or warm the chicken and quinoa 60 seconds, then add the cold toppings.
