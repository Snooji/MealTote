# Crustless Ham, Broccoli & Cheddar Egg-White Quiche

_No free-licensed photo yet; the app shows the MealTote logo._

**Breakfast** · American comfort · 55 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> All the comfort of a cheesy quiche without the crust: tender broccoli and smoky ham baked in a light custard of eggs, egg whites and cottage cheese with sharp cheddar on top. Slice it into wedges for the week.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups liquid egg whites
- 4 large eggs
- 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
- 6 oz sliced ham, diced
- 12 oz broccoli, in small florets
- ¾ cup shredded sharp cheddar
- 3 green onions, sliced

From the pantry: cooking spray, ½ tsp dried thyme, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp pepper, pinch of nutmeg

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F and coat a deep 9-inch pie dish with cooking spray.
2. Cook the broccoli florets in boiling salted water for 1 minute, drain and rinse under cold water. Pat dry and spread in the dish with the ham and green onions.
3. Whisk the eggs, egg whites, cottage cheese, thyme, salt, pepper and nutmeg vigorously to break up the cottage cheese curds. Stir in half the cheddar.
4. Pour the egg mixture over the filling and scatter the rest of the cheddar on top.
5. Bake until puffed, golden and just set in the center (a knife comes out clean), 35 to 40 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before cutting into 4 wedges.

**Tip:** Blot the broccoli well. Wet vegetables leak water into a crustless quiche and make the bottom soggy.

Adapted from [Cookin Canuck](https://www.cookincanuck.com/broccoli-cheddar-quiche-crustless/).

## Storing

Fridge 5 days · freezer up to 2 months. Refrigerate wedges covered; microwave 60 to 90 seconds, or reheat at 350°F for 10 minutes.
