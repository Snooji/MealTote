# Breakfast Banana Splits with Vanilla Protein Cottage Cheese

**Breakfast** · American comfort · 10 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Dessert for breakfast, with a protein payoff: a split banana filled with scoops of vanilla-whipped cottage cheese and Greek yogurt, then topped like a sundae with strawberries, granola and a few chocolate chips.

## Ingredients

- 4 bananas
- 3 cups low-fat cottage cheese
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 scoops vanilla whey protein powder
- 8 oz strawberries, sliced
- ⅓ cup almond granola
- 1 tbsp mini chocolate chips

From the pantry: 1 tsp vanilla extract, pinch of cinnamon

## Steps

1. Whisk the Greek yogurt, protein powder and vanilla until smooth, then fold in the cottage cheese.
2. Peel the bananas, split each in half lengthwise and lay the halves in a long dish or shallow bowl.
3. Scoop the cottage cheese mixture into 3 mounds between the banana halves, like ice cream.
4. Top with strawberries, granola, chocolate chips and a pinch of cinnamon, and eat right away.

**Tip:** If you don't like the curds in cottage cheese, blend it smooth first; it turns thick and creamy, almost like soft-serve.

Adapted from [Live Eat Learn](https://www.liveeatlearn.com/breakfast-banana-split/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer not recommended. Make the vanilla cottage cheese up to 3 days ahead; split the bananas just before serving.
