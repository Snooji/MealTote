# Frango Piri-Piri (Portuguese Chili-Roast Chicken & Potatoes)

> Portuguese churrasqueira chicken at home: a whole bird spatchcocked and soaked in a fiery garlic, chili, lemon and paprika marinade, then roasted until lacquered and crisp alongside golden potatoes.

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken (about 4½ lb)
- 3 hot red or green chiles (jalapeño or Fresno)
- 6 garlic cloves
- 2 lemons, juiced
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved
- 5 oz salad greens

From the pantry: ⅓ cup red wine vinegar, 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp dried thyme, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp red pepper flakes, 2 tsp salt

## Steps

1. Blend the chiles, garlic, vinegar, lemon juice, paprika, oregano, thyme, sugar, pepper flakes, salt and 2 tbsp olive oil into a smooth marinade. Save 3 tbsp for serving.
2. Cut out the chicken's backbone with kitchen shears and press it flat. Cut a few deep slashes into the legs and breast, then rub the marinade all over and into the cuts. Chill at least 4 hours or overnight.
3. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the potatoes with the remaining olive oil and salt, spread them in a roasting pan and set the chicken skin side up on top.
4. Roast until the skin is deeply browned and the thickest part of the thigh reaches 170°F, 50 to 60 minutes. Rest 10 minutes.
5. Carve, spoon the pan juices and reserved marinade over the chicken, and serve with the potatoes and a simple green salad.

**Tip:** Grilling over medium-indirect heat for 40 to 50 minutes is the traditional way. Real piri-piri chiles (African bird's eye) are hotter; add a dried chile de árbol or two if you want more fire.

Adapted from [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/piri-piri-chicken).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Reheat in a 375°F oven for 10 to 15 minutes; the meat is also great cold in sandwiches.
