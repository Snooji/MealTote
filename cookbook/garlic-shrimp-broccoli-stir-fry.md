# Garlic Shrimp & Broccoli Stir-Fry over Jasmine Rice

> Plump shrimp and bright, crisp broccoli tossed in a glossy garlic-ginger sauce of soy, oyster sauce and honey. A 25-minute stir-fry that beats delivery.

## Ingredients

- 2¼ lb large shrimp, peeled
- 1½ lb broccoli florets
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- ½ cup chicken broth
- 1 cup rice

From the pantry: 1 tbsp cornstarch, 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp sesame oil, ¼ tsp white pepper, salt

## Steps

1. Cook the rice. Whisk the broth, soy sauce, oyster sauce, honey, cornstarch, sesame oil and white pepper.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Stir-fry the broccoli with 2 tbsp water until bright green and crisp-tender, 3 to 4 minutes. Remove.
3. Add the remaining oil and the shrimp in a single layer. Sear until pink on one side, 1½ minutes, flip, and add the garlic and ginger.
4. Return the broccoli, stir the sauce and pour it in. Toss until the sauce thickens and coats everything, about 1 minute.
5. Serve right away over rice.

**Tip:** Pat the shrimp very dry before they go in the pan; wet shrimp release water and the sauce never gets glossy.

Adapted from [Jo Cooks](https://www.jocooks.com/recipes/shrimp-broccoli-stir-fry/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer not recommended. Reheat 60 to 90 seconds at 70% power so the shrimp don't toughen.
