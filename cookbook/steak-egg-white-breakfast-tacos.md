# Sirloin Steak & Egg Breakfast Tacos with Salsa Verde

**Breakfast** · Tex-Mex · 30 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Seared, chili-rubbed sirloin sliced thin and tucked into warm corn tortillas with soft-scrambled eggs, tangy salsa verde, avocado and cilantro. Weekend-brunch good, and leaner than it tastes.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb top sirloin steak, about ¾ inch thick
- 2 cups liquid egg whites
- 2 large eggs
- 8 corn tortillas
- ½ cup salsa verde
- 1 avocado, diced
- ¼ small onion, finely diced
- a handful of cilantro, chopped
- 1 lime, in wedges

From the pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Pat the steak dry and rub with the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, ¾ tsp salt and pepper.
2. Heat 2 tsp of the oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Sear the steak 4 to 5 minutes per side for medium-rare (130°F inside), then rest it on a board for 5 minutes and slice thinly across the grain.
3. Lower the heat to medium-low and add the rest of the oil. Whisk the egg whites and eggs with a pinch of salt, pour into the pan and gently push them around with a spatula until softly set, 3 to 4 minutes.
4. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or directly over a gas flame until soft and lightly charred, about 30 seconds per side, and stack them in a towel.
5. Fill each tortilla with eggs and steak, then top with salsa verde, avocado, onion and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges.

**Tip:** Double up the tortillas if yours are thin, as taquerías do, so they don't split. Slicing the steak across the grain is what keeps lean sirloin tender.

Adapted from [Missouri Beef Industry Council](https://www.mobeef.org/recipes/recipe/5284/steak-and-eggs-breakfast-tacos).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer not recommended. Store steak and eggs separately; reheat briefly in a skillet and warm fresh tortillas.
