# Huli Huli Chicken with Pineapple

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Dinner** · Hawaiian · 40 min · serves 4 · skill 1/3

> Hawaii's sweet-savory pineapple-soy-ginger glazed chicken, grilled or broiled until sticky, with charred pineapple and coconut rice.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless thighs
- 1 pineapple (juice ½ cup, slice the rest)
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup brown sugar or honey
- 2-inch ginger
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1½ cups jasmine rice
- green onions

From the pantry: ¼ cup ketchup, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, salt

## Steps

1. Whisk the pineapple juice, soy, sugar, ketchup, vinegar, ginger and garlic. Reserve ½ cup for basting and marinate the chicken in the rest for at least 30 minutes.
2. Cook the rice in the coconut milk plus water to make 2¼ cups liquid, with a pinch of salt.
3. Simmer the reserved sauce until syrupy, 5 minutes.
4. Grill or broil the chicken 5 to 6 minutes per side, basting with the thickened sauce, until charred and sticky. Grill the pineapple slices alongside.
5. Serve with coconut rice, pineapple and green onions.

**Tip:** 'Huli' means turn. Flip often and keep basting.


## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Reheat at 375°F or microwave 2 minutes.
