# BBQ Chicken Stuffed Sweet Potatoes with Yogurt Slaw

> Soft, caramelized baked sweet potatoes piled high with smoky shredded BBQ chicken and melted cheddar, topped with a tangy, crunchy yogurt slaw and green onions.

## Ingredients

- 4 medium sweet potatoes (1½ lb)
- 2 lb boneless chicken breasts
- ⅔ cup BBQ sauce
- ½ cup shredded cheddar
- 2 cups coleslaw mix
- ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp minced red onion
- 3 green onions, sliced
- a handful of cilantro

From the pantry: 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tbsp cider vinegar, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Prick the sweet potatoes and bake on a foil-lined pan until very soft, 45 to 50 minutes.
2. Meanwhile season the chicken with the paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Put it in a pot, cover with water, bring to a simmer, then cover and cook gently until 165°F inside, 15 to 18 minutes. Shred with two forks.
3. Toss the shredded chicken with the BBQ sauce in a skillet over medium heat until hot and sticky, 3 minutes.
4. Stir the yogurt, vinegar, onion and a pinch of salt and pepper, then toss with the coleslaw mix.
5. Split the potatoes, fill with BBQ chicken, top with cheddar and broil 2 minutes to melt. Finish with slaw, green onions and cilantro.

**Tip:** Microwave the potatoes 8 to 10 minutes on busy nights, then crisp the skins in a 425°F oven for 10 minutes.

Adapted from [Foodness Gracious](https://foodnessgracious.com/recipes/bbq-chicken-stuffed-sweet-potatoes).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 3 months. Store slaw separately. Microwave the stuffed potatoes 2 to 3 minutes.
