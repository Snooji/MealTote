# Kuku Sabzi (Persian Herb Frittata) with Yogurt & Walnuts

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Lunch** · Persian · 45 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> More herbs than egg: piles of parsley, cilantro, dill and green onion bound with just enough egg, studded with walnuts and fried into a deep green, crisp-edged cake. Eaten warm or cold with yogurt and bread.

## Ingredients

- 7 eggs
- 2 bunches parsley, finely chopped
- 2 bunches cilantro, finely chopped
- 1 bunch dill, finely chopped
- 1 bunch green onions, finely chopped
- ½ cup walnuts, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- ½ cucumber, grated
- 4 pitas or flatbreads

From the pantry: 5 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp baking powder, 1 tsp dried mint, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Wash the herbs and dry them very well, then chop finely (a food processor in short pulses is fine). You want about 6 packed cups.
2. Warm 1 tbsp oil in a 10-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat and cook the green onions and herbs, stirring, until darker and dry, about 5 minutes. Cool 10 minutes.
3. Beat the eggs with the turmeric, baking powder, garlic, 1½ tsp salt and pepper. Fold in the herbs and walnuts.
4. Heat 3 tbsp oil in the skillet over medium heat, pour in the mixture and smooth the top. Cover and cook over medium-low until the bottom is browned and the top nearly set, 12 to 15 minutes.
5. Slide it onto a plate, flip it back into the pan and cook the second side uncovered 5 minutes (or finish in a 375°F oven for 10 minutes instead of flipping).
6. Stir the yogurt with the cucumber, mint, the last 1 tbsp oil and salt. Cut the kuku in wedges and serve with the yogurt and warm bread.

**Tip:** Dry herbs are the secret: wet herbs make a soggy kuku. A spoonful of dried barberries is the classic tart touch if you can find them at a Persian market.

Adapted from [Aish (Jamie Geller)](https://aish.com/wprm_print/kuku-sabzi-persian-green-frittata).

## Storing

Fridge 4 days · freezer up to 2 months. Great cold or at room temperature in a wrap. Rewarm wedges in a skillet or 350°F oven for 8 minutes.
