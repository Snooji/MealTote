# Beef Suya Skewers with Onion-Tomato Salad

**Dinner** · Nigerian & West African · 50 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> Nigeria's beloved street-grill skewers: thin beef caked in yaji, a smoky, peppery ground-peanut rub, charred hot and piled with raw onion, tomato and cucumber.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb sirloin or flank steak, sliced ¼ inch thick
- ¾ cup roasted unsalted peanuts
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 Roma tomatoes, sliced
- ½ English cucumber, sliced
- 1½ cups rice

From the pantry: 2 tbsp oil, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp cayenne, 1 tsp ground ginger, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 beef bouillon cube, salt

## Steps

1. Soak 10 to 12 wooden skewers in water. Start the rice.
2. Make the yaji: pulse the peanuts in a food processor or spice grinder with the paprika, cayenne, ginger, garlic powder, onion powder, crumbled bouillon and 1 tsp salt to a fine, sandy powder. Stop before it turns into butter.
3. Toss the beef with the oil and a pinch of salt, then with about two-thirds of the yaji until well coated. Rest 20 minutes (or up to overnight in the fridge).
4. Thread the beef onto the skewers in folds. Heat the oven broiler with the rack 4 inches from the heat, or heat a grill to high. Cook the skewers on a foil-lined sheet until charred at the edges and just cooked, 3 to 4 minutes per side.
5. Pile onto a platter with the onion, tomato and cucumber, dust with the remaining yaji and serve with the rice.

**Tip:** Freeze the steak for 30 minutes before slicing; it firms up and slices paper-thin, which is how suya stays tender over high heat.

Adapted from [Chef Lola's Kitchen](https://cheflolaskitchen.com/suya/).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 2 months. Store meat and salad separately. Reheat skewers in a hot skillet 1 to 2 minutes per side.
