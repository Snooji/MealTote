# Sabzi Polo ba Mahi (Persian Herbed Rice with Crisp Turmeric Salmon)

_Illustrated in the app (no free-licensed photo yet)._

**Dinner** · Persian · 60 min · serves 4 · skill 2/3

> The Persian New Year plate: basmati steamed with heaps of parsley, cilantro, dill, green onion and garlic until fragrant and fluffy, topped with saffron rice and served beside turmeric-crusted pan-fried fish.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ lb salmon fillets, in 4 pieces
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 1 bunch parsley, finely chopped
- 1 bunch cilantro, finely chopped
- 1 bunch dill, finely chopped
- 1 bunch green onions, finely chopped
- 6 garlic cloves, chopped
- a big pinch of saffron
- 2 lemons, in wedges

From the pantry: 5 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, 2 tbsp flour, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Rinse the rice several times until the water runs clear. Steep the saffron in 3 tbsp hot water. Mix the chopped herbs, fenugreek and garlic.
2. Boil the rice in a large pot of well-salted water until just tender outside but still firm in the center, 6 to 7 minutes, then drain.
3. Wipe out the pot, add 2 tbsp oil and 2 tbsp water, then layer the rice and herbs in a mound. Poke a few holes with a spoon handle, drizzle with 1 tbsp oil, wrap the lid in a towel and cover. Cook on medium 5 minutes, then very low 35 minutes, until fluffy with a golden crust at the bottom.
4. While the rice steams, season the salmon with the turmeric, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper and dust lightly with the flour. Fry in 2 tbsp oil over medium-high heat until crisp, 3 to 4 minutes a side.
5. Toss a cup of rice with the saffron water. Mound the herb rice on a platter, top with the saffron rice and the crust broken into pieces, and serve with the fish and lemon.

**Tip:** The traditional fish is Caspian whitefish or sea bass, and bitter orange is squeezed over it; cod or tilapia and lemon work fine. Dry the herbs well before chopping so the rice stays fluffy.

Adapted from [Hami Sharafi](https://www.hamisharafi.com/free-recipes/sabzi-polo-ba-mahi).

## Storing

Fridge 3 days · freezer up to 1 months. Store rice and fish separately. Reheat rice covered with a sprinkle of water; warm the fish in a 325°F oven.
