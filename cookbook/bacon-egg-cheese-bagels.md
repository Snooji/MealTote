# Bacon, Egg & Cheese Bagel Sandwiches

## Ingredients

- 4 plain or everything bagels, split
- 6 large eggs
- 6 slices thick-cut bacon, halved
- 4 slices sharp cheddar
- 1 tbsp butter

From the pantry: 1 tbsp milk, ¼ tsp garlic powder, salt, pepper, ketchup or hot sauce to serve

## Steps

1. Lay the bacon in a cold large skillet and cook over medium heat, turning now and then, until crisp, 8 to 10 minutes. Drain on paper towels and pour off all but a thin film of fat.
2. While the bacon cooks, toast the bagels until golden at the edges.
3. Whisk the eggs with the milk, garlic powder and a pinch each of salt and pepper.
4. Melt the butter in the skillet over medium-low heat. Pour in the eggs and let them set for 30 seconds, then gently push them toward the center until just set but still glossy, about 2 minutes. Fold into a rough rectangle and cut into 4 pieces.
5. Lay a slice of cheddar on each warm egg portion so it starts to melt, then stack on the bagel bottoms with 3 half slices of bacon each. Close up and serve with ketchup or hot sauce.

**Tip:** Pull a little of the soft bread out of the top half of each bagel so the filling sits snugly and the sandwich is easier to bite.

Adapted from [Cafe Delites](https://cafedelites.com/10-minute-bagel-breakfast/).

## Storing

Fridge 2 days · freezer up to 1 months. Wrap cooled sandwiches in foil and freeze. Reheat unwrapped in a paper towel, 1½ minutes in the microwave, then 2 minutes in a toaster oven.
