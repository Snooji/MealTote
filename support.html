Keep it free for everyone

Most apps that help you plan meals and shopping lock the useful parts behind a subscription. I wanted one that does the whole job for free, so I built MealTote: the questions, the week of meals, the grocery list split across your stores, the prep guide and the cookbook PDF, with no ads and no paywall.
