Meal prep plans

Meal prep works best with food that tastes as good on Thursday as on Sunday. MealTote's prep guide batches the week's chopping, sauces, marinades and grains into one or two sessions.

These recipes keep at least four days in the fridge, and many freeze well: chilis, curries, grain bowls, casseroles, soups and breakfast burritos.
