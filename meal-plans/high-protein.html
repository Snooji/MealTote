High-protein meal plans

Eating enough protein to build muscle is easier with a plan. MealTote builds a week of breakfasts, lunches and dinners around your protein, carb and fat targets, and sizes every portion to match.

These recipes get at least 30% of their calories from protein: egg-white breakfast burritos, protein pancakes, chicken and rice bowls, lean beef and turkey dinners, salmon and shrimp. Set a target like 190 g a day and the planner leans on them, then suggests extra protein or a side when a meal falls short.

Questions

How much protein is in a MealTote high-protein plan?

With a 190 g target on about 2,400 calories, a week of planned meals averages 190 to 200 g of protein a day.

Can I set my own macros?

Yes. Add protein, carb and fat targets for each person and portions are sized to match.

Is MealTote free?

Yes. MealTote is free with no ads and no premium tier, and you can use it without an account.
