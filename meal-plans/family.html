Family meal plans

Planning for a family means feeding different appetites from the same pot. Add everyone you cook for and MealTote sizes each person's portion from one recipe.

These are familiar, easy dishes most kids will eat: pastas, tacos, casseroles, chicken dinners, pancakes and sandwiches, all with simple steps and normal supermarket ingredients.

How MealTote builds your plan

  • Answer a few questions: who's eating, calories, optional macros, foods you love, allergies, time and budget.
  • Get a 3, 5 or 7-day plan with portions sized for each person, and swap any meal in one tap.
  • Shop from one grocery list split across Walmart, Aldi, Costco, Kroger and 30+ other chains, with a meal prep guide for the week.
