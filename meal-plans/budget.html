Budget meal plans

Feeding a household on a budget starts with knowing what the week will cost. MealTote prices every recipe at the stores you choose and splits your grocery list for the lowest total.

These recipes come in around $2.50 a serving or less: hearty soups, rice and bean dishes, pasta, sheet-pan dinners and egg breakfasts. Set a weekly budget and the planner keeps the plan under it.

Build my free budget meal plan

How MealTote builds your plan

  • Answer a few questions: who's eating, calories, optional macros, foods you love, allergies, time and budget.
  • Get a 3, 5 or 7-day plan with portions sized for each person, and swap any meal in one tap.
  • Shop from one grocery list split across Walmart, Aldi, Costco, Kroger and 30+ other chains, with a meal prep guide for the week.

Questions

Where do the prices come from?

Typical shelf prices at 36 US chains, including Walmart, Aldi, Costco and Kroger. They're estimates, so check in-store.

Can it split my list between two stores?

Yes. Choose lowest total or fewest stops and MealTote assigns each item to a store.

Is MealTote free?

Yes. MealTote is free with no ads and no premium tier, and you can use it without an account.
