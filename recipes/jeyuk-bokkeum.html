Jeyuk Bokkeum (Spicy Gochujang Pork) with Lettuce Wraps

DinnerKorean35 minServes 4900 kcal37 g protein

Korea's go-to spicy pork stir-fry: thin slices of pork shoulder coated in a gingery gochujang marinade and seared over high heat until caramelized. Wrap it in lettuce with rice, or just pile it on the rice.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless pork shoulder, sliced ⅛ inch thin
  • 5 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 4 green onions, in 2-inch pieces
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 head butter lettuce, leaves separated
  • 1½ cups short-grain rice

From your pantry: 1 tbsp gochugaru, 2 tbsp mirin, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp sesame oil, ½ tsp black pepper, 2 tbsp oil.

Steps

  1. Start the rice. Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, soy, mirin, 1½ tbsp sugar, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, sesame seeds and pepper into a paste.
  2. Separate the pork slices and massage them with the paste until evenly coated. Let them marinate at least 15 minutes, or up to a day in the fridge.
  3. Heat the oil in a large cast-iron skillet or wok over high. Sprinkle in the remaining ½ tbsp sugar and let it caramelize for a few seconds, then add the green onions and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add half the pork and onion and stir-fry until the pork is cooked through and caramelized at the edges, 4 to 5 minutes. Repeat with the rest so the pan stays hot.
  5. Serve with rice and lettuce leaves for wrapping.

Tip: Freeze the pork for 30 minutes before slicing so you can cut it thin. Many Korean and Asian markets also sell pre-sliced pork shoulder for bulgogi.
