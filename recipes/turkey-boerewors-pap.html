Turkey Boerewors Patties with Pap & Chakalaka

DinnerSouth African50 minServes 4905 kcal46 g protein

Homemade turkey sausage seasoned like boerewors with toasted coriander, nutmeg and cloves, with soft, buttery pap and chakalaka, South Africa's spicy pepper-and-bean relish.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground turkey
  • 2 cups white or fine yellow cornmeal
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 bell peppers, chopped
  • 2 large carrots, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 jalapeño, minced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 can (16 oz) baked beans in tomato sauce

From your pantry: 3 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp ground coriander, ½ tsp nutmeg, ¼ tsp ground cloves, ¼ tsp allspice, 1 tbsp vinegar, 1 tbsp curry powder, salt, pepper.

Instructions

  1. Toast the coriander in a dry skillet for 30 seconds. Mix it into the turkey with the nutmeg, cloves, allspice, vinegar, 1¼ tsp salt and ½ tsp pepper. Shape 8 thin patties and chill while you cook.
  2. For the pap, bring 5 cups salted water to a boil, whisk in the cornmeal gradually, then cover and cook on low, stirring well every 5 minutes, until thick and smooth, 20 to 25 minutes. Stir in the butter.
  3. For the chakalaka, heat 1 tbsp oil in a pot over medium. Cook the onion, peppers and carrots 6 minutes, add the garlic, ginger, jalapeño and curry powder for 1 minute, then the tomatoes and baked beans. Simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high and cook the patties until browned and 165°F inside, 4 to 5 minutes per side.
  5. Serve the patties on the pap with plenty of chakalaka spooned over.

Tip: Real boerewors is a coiled beef-and-pork sausage; to make links, pipe this mixture into casings or just roll it into long logs before pan-frying. White maize meal makes the most authentic pap.
