Gilgeori Toast (Korean Street Egg & Cabbage Sandwich)

BreakfastKorean25 minServes 4660 kcal24 g protein

Photo: gildedgingerbread · CC BY 4.0 · source

Seoul's famous street-cart breakfast: a fluffy cabbage-and-carrot omelet tucked between butter-toasted bread with melty cheese, a sprinkle of sugar and a zigzag of ketchup and mayo.

Ingredients

  • 8 slices brioche or soft white bread
  • 3 cups coleslaw mix (cabbage and carrot)
  • 6 eggs
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 3 tbsp butter

From your pantry: 3 tbsp ketchup, 2 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp sugar, salt.

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs with ½ tsp salt, then stir in the coleslaw mix and green onions until everything is coated.
  2. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a large nonstick skillet over medium. Toast the bread in batches until golden on one side, 1 to 2 minutes, adding butter as needed. Set aside.
  3. Add the last of the butter and spoon the egg mixture into 4 rough squares about the size of the bread. Cook until set and lightly browned, 2 to 3 minutes per side.
  4. Lay a cheese slice on each omelet so it starts to melt, then put each on the untoasted side of a slice of bread.
  5. Sprinkle with a little sugar, zigzag with ketchup and mayo, top with the second slice toasted side out, and cut in half.

Tip: The sprinkle of sugar sounds odd but it's the signature. It plays off the salty egg and tangy ketchup the way it does at the street stalls.
