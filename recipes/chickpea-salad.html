Mediterranean Chickpea Salad

LunchGreek15 minServes 4360 kcal14 g protein

A crunchy, lemony no-cook salad of chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes and feta that gets better as it sits. Packs perfectly.

Ingredients

  • 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ onion, finely diced
  • ½ cup feta crumbles
  • 1 lemon, juiced

From your pantry: 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried oregano, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Whisk the lemon juice, olive oil, oregano, ½ tsp salt and pepper in a large bowl.
  2. Soak the onion in the dressing for 5 minutes to soften its bite.
  3. Add the chickpeas, cucumber and tomatoes and toss well.
  4. Fold in the feta.
  5. Eat right away or refrigerate up to 3 days.

Tip: Spoon it over spring mix or into a pita to stretch it into a bigger lunch.
