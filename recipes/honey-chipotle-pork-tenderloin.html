Honey-Chipotle Pork Tenderloin with Black Bean & Corn Salsa

DinnerTex-Mex40 minServes 4555 kcal68 g protein

Pork tenderloin brushed with a smoky, sweet honey-chipotle-orange glaze and roasted until juicy, sliced over a hearty black bean, corn and tomato salsa with lime and cilantro.

Ingredients

  • 2 pork tenderloins (2½ lb)
  • 2 chipotles in adobo plus 1 tbsp sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 orange, juiced
  • 1 can black beans, rinsed
  • 1½ cups corn, charred or thawed
  • 1 Roma tomato, diced
  • ¼ red onion, finely diced
  • ½ bunch cilantro, chopped
  • 2 limes
  • 1 jalapeño, minced

From your pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp cumin, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Blend the chipotles, adobo, honey, orange juice, Dijon and garlic until smooth.
  2. Season the pork with cumin, 1 tsp salt and pepper. Heat the oil in an ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat and brown the pork all over, 5 to 6 minutes.
  3. Brush with half the glaze and roast 10 minutes, brush again and roast until 145°F inside, 5 to 8 minutes more. Rest 5 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile char the corn in a dry skillet over high heat, 4 minutes, then toss with the beans, tomato, onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice and salt.
  5. Slice the pork and serve over the salsa, drizzled with the pan juices.

Tip: Honey glazes burn easily; brush it on in two rounds instead of all at once.
