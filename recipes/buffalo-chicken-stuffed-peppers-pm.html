Buffalo Chicken Stuffed Peppers with Melted Mozzarella

DinnerAmerican comfort50 minServes 4580 kcal67 g protein

Sweet bell pepper halves baked until tender and stuffed with creamy, tangy buffalo chicken and a little rice, then topped with bubbling mozzarella, feta and green onions.

Ingredients

  • 4 large bell peppers, halved and seeded
  • 2 lb boneless chicken breasts
  • ½ cup buffalo wing sauce
  • ⅔ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 oz cream cheese, softened
  • ½ cup rice
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • ¼ cup crumbled feta or blue cheese
  • 3 green onions, sliced

From your pantry: 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, ½ tsp dried dill, cooking spray, salt, pepper.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Mist the pepper halves with cooking spray, season with salt and bake cut-side up on a sheet pan for 12 minutes to soften. Cook the rice.
  2. Meanwhile put the chicken in a pot, cover with water, add 1 tsp salt and simmer gently, covered, until 165°F inside, 15 to 18 minutes. Shred with two forks.
  3. Mix the shredded chicken with the buffalo sauce, yogurt, cream cheese, rice, garlic powder, onion powder and dill.
  4. Pack the filling into the peppers, top with mozzarella and bake until the cheese is bubbly and the peppers are tender, 15 to 18 minutes.
  5. Finish with feta and green onions, and pass extra buffalo sauce at the table.

Tip: Rotisserie or leftover chicken works perfectly here; you need about 5 cups shredded.
