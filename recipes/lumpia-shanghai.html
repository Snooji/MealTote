Crispy Lumpia Shanghai with Sweet & Sour Sauce

DinnerFilipino60 minServes 4930 kcal32 g protein

Pencil-thin, shatteringly crisp Filipino spring rolls packed with seasoned pork, carrot and water chestnut, the first thing to vanish at any Filipino party. Served with a quick sweet and sour sauce and rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 13 lumpia or spring roll wrappers, cut in half
  • 2 carrots, finely minced
  • 1 onion, finely minced
  • ½ can water chestnuts, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 egg, plus water to seal
  • 1½ tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 cup jasmine rice
  • 1 cucumber, sliced, for the side

From your pantry: oil for frying, 3 tbsp ketchup, 3 tbsp sugar, ¼ cup white vinegar, 1 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tsp sesame oil, ½ tsp black pepper, salt.

Steps

  1. Mix the pork, carrots, onion, water chestnuts, garlic, egg, soy sauce, sesame oil, pepper and ½ tsp salt until evenly combined.
  2. Lay a half wrapper with a long edge toward you, spread a thin line of filling (about 1 tbsp) along it, fold in the sides and roll up tightly. Seal the end with a dab of water. Repeat to make about 26 rolls.
  3. For the sauce, simmer the ketchup, sugar, vinegar and ½ cup water in a small pan, then stir in the cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water and cook until glossy, 1 minute. Start the rice.
  4. Heat 1 inch of oil in a deep skillet to 350°F. Fry the lumpia in batches, turning, until deep golden and crisp, 4 to 5 minutes; the filling cooks through in that time. Drain on a rack.
  5. Serve hot with the sauce and rice.

Tip: Roll them as thin as a cigar so the pork cooks through before the wrapper burns. Uncooked rolls freeze beautifully on a tray; fry them from frozen, adding 2 minutes.
