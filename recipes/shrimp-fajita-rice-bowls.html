Shrimp Fajita Bowls with Cilantro-Lime Rice

LunchTex-Mex30 minServes 4505 kcal57 g protein

Photo: mild_swearwords · CC BY-SA 2.0 · source

Chili-lime shrimp and charred peppers and onions over cilantro-lime rice and black beans, finished with fresh pico and a lime crema. Fajita night in a meal-prep container.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb large shrimp, peeled
  • 3 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 red or yellow onion, sliced
  • 2 limes
  • ¾ cup rice
  • ½ can black beans, rinsed
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, minced
  • ½ bunch cilantro, chopped
  • ⅔ cup plain Greek yogurt

From your pantry: 2 tbsp oil, 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt.

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice and fluff it with half the cilantro, the juice of 1 lime and a pinch of salt. Warm the black beans.
  2. Mix the tomatoes, jalapeño, a little cilantro and a pinch of salt for pico. Stir the yogurt with a squeeze of lime and salt for crema.
  3. Toss the shrimp with 1 tbsp oil, the juice of ½ lime, the spices and ½ tsp salt.
  4. Heat the remaining oil in a large skillet over high heat. Cook the peppers and onion until charred at the edges but still crisp, 6 to 7 minutes, then move them to the side. Add the shrimp in one layer and cook until pink, about 2 minutes per side.
  5. Divide the rice and beans among bowls and top with the fajita vegetables, shrimp, pico and crema.

Tip: Get the skillet really hot before the peppers go in; a little char is what gives fajitas their flavor.
