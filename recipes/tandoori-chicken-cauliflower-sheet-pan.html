Sheet-Pan Tandoori Chicken with Cauliflower & Chickpeas

DinnerNorth Indian50 minServes 4660 kcal75 g protein

Photo: stu_spivack · CC BY-SA 2.0 · source

Chicken in a tangy, brick-red tandoori yogurt marinade roasted on one pan with spiced cauliflower, chickpeas and red onion, served over basmati with a cool yogurt raita.

Ingredients

  • 2¼ lb boneless chicken breasts, in large chunks
  • 1⅓ cups plain Greek yogurt, divided
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1½ tbsp grated ginger
  • 4 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 head cauliflower, in florets
  • ½ can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 small red onion, in wedges
  • ¾ cup basmati rice
  • ½ bunch cilantro, chopped

From your pantry: 2 tbsp oil, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp cayenne, ¼ tsp cinnamon, salt.

Instructions

  1. Mix ¾ cup yogurt with the lemon juice, ginger, garlic, garam masala, paprika, turmeric, cumin, cayenne, cinnamon and 1½ tsp salt. Coat the chicken and marinate 30 minutes or up to overnight.
  2. Heat the oven to 450°F. Toss the cauliflower, chickpeas and onion with the oil, a spoon of the marinade and ½ tsp salt on a large sheet pan.
  3. Nestle the chicken among the vegetables. Roast until the chicken is 165°F inside and the cauliflower is tender and browned, 22 to 25 minutes. Broil 2 to 3 minutes for char.
  4. Cook the rice. Stir the remaining yogurt with a pinch of salt and some cilantro for a quick raita.
  5. Serve the chicken and vegetables over rice with raita and cilantro.

Tip: Use two pans if needed; crowded cauliflower steams instead of roasting and you lose the charred bits.
