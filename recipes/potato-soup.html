Loaded Baked Potato Soup

DinnerAmerican comfort45 minServes 6590 kcal27 g protein

Thick, velvety potato soup loaded like the best baked potato: crispy bacon, sharp cheddar, sour cream and green onions.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb russet potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 12 oz bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • green onions

From your pantry: 3 tbsp flour, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Cook the bacon in a large pot until crisp. Remove, leaving 3 tbsp fat.
  2. Cook the onion in the fat until soft, then the garlic and flour for 1 minute.
  3. Add the potatoes and broth and simmer until very tender, 15 minutes.
  4. Mash about half the potatoes right in the pot for a thick, chunky texture. Stir in the milk, half the cheddar and half the sour cream and season.
  5. Ladle into bowls and pile on bacon, cheese, sour cream and green onions.

Tip: Russets break down into a naturally creamy soup. Waxy potatoes stay too firm.
