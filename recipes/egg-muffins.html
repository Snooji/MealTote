Spinach, Feta & Pepper Egg Muffins

BreakfastAmerican comfort30 minServes 6205 kcal16 g protein

Photo: fir0002 · GFDL 1.2 · source

Twelve fluffy, veggie-packed egg bites that reheat in 30 seconds. Bake once on Sunday and breakfast is handled until Friday.

Ingredients

  • 12 eggs
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 2 big handfuls baby spinach, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, finely diced
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • ½ cup feta crumbles

From your pantry: Cooking spray, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and spray a 12-cup muffin tin well.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, ½ tsp salt and plenty of pepper.
  3. Divide the spinach, pepper, green onions and feta among the cups.
  4. Pour the eggs over the vegetables, filling each cup about ¾ full.
  5. Bake 20 to 22 minutes, until puffed and just set in the center. Cool 5 minutes before lifting out.

Tip: Keep them in the fridge for 5 days, or freeze and microwave from frozen for 60 seconds.
