Csirkepaprikás (Hungarian Chicken Paprikash)

DinnerHungarian65 minServes 4735 kcal58 g protein

Tender chicken thighs simmered in a brick-red sauce of slow-cooked onion and sweet paprika, finished with tangy sour cream and spooned over buttery egg noodles.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless chicken thighs, halved
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, seeded and diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • ¾ cup sour cream, at room temperature
  • 9 oz wide egg noodles
  • chopped parsley

From your pantry: 3 tbsp sweet Hungarian paprika, 2 tbsp butter or lard, 2 tbsp flour, 1½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper.

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Melt the butter in a Dutch oven over medium-high and brown the chicken in batches, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Set aside.
  2. Lower the heat to medium, add the onions and cook until soft and golden, 8 to 10 minutes. Add the garlic, tomatoes and pepper and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the paprika (it scorches easily). Return to the heat, add the broth and chicken, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer gently until the chicken is very tender, 30 minutes.
  4. Cook the noodles in salted water until tender; drain.
  5. Whisk the flour into the sour cream until smooth, whisk in a ladle of the hot sauce, then stir it all back into the pot. Simmer gently, stirring, until thickened, 2 to 3 minutes. Serve over the noodles with parsley.

Tip: Use real imported sweet Hungarian paprika and a fresh tin: it's the whole flavor of the dish. In Hungary it's served with nokedli, short spätzle-like dumplings, if you want to go all in.
