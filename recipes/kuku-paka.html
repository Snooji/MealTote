Kuku Paka (Kenyan Coastal Coconut Chicken Curry)

DinnerKenyan & East African55 minServes 4855 kcal54 g protein

Charred chicken simmered in a creamy coconut sauce built on a blended onion, chili, ginger and garlic paste, from Kenya's Swahili coast. Rich, bright and served with rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
  • 2 onions, roughly chopped
  • 2 green chilies or jalapeños
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1-inch piece ginger
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • ½ bunch cilantro, chopped
  • 1½ cups basmati rice

From your pantry: 2 tbsp oil, 2 tbsp curry powder, 1 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp salt.

Steps

  1. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp oil, the turmeric, half the lemon juice and ½ tsp salt. Broil 4 inches from the heat until charred in spots, 5 to 6 minutes per side (it finishes in the sauce). Cut into chunks.
  2. Blend the onions, chilies, garlic and ginger with ¼ cup water into a smooth paste. Start the rice.
  3. Heat the remaining oil in a deep skillet over medium. Cook the paste with the curry powder and cumin, stirring often, until it darkens and stops smelling raw, 10 minutes.
  4. Add the tomatoes and cook 5 minutes, then the coconut milk and chicken. Simmer gently, uncovered, until the sauce is thick and the chicken is 175°F inside, 12 to 15 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and the rest of the lemon juice, top with cilantro and serve over rice.

Tip: On the coast the chicken is grilled over charcoal first; that smoky char is what makes kuku paka special, so don't skip the broiler step.
