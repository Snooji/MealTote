Danish Æggekage (Fluffy Egg Cake with Bacon & Tomatoes)

BreakfastScandinavian30 minServes 4510 kcal32 g protein

A thick, airy Danish skillet omelet cooked gently under a lid, studded with tomatoes and chives and served with crisp bacon strips on dark rye bread.

Ingredients

  • 12 large eggs
  • ⅔ cup milk
  • 3 Roma tomatoes, in chunks
  • a bunch of chives or green onion tops, chopped
  • 1½ tbsp butter
  • 8 oz thick-cut bacon
  • 4 slices dark rye bread

From your pantry: ½ tsp salt, pepper.

Instructions

  1. Cook the bacon in a large skillet over medium until crisp, 8 to 10 minutes. Drain on paper towels and keep warm. Wipe out the pan.
  2. Whisk the eggs hard for a full minute (or use a mixer) until frothy, then whisk in the milk, salt and pepper.
  3. Melt the butter in the 10- to 12-inch skillet over medium, pour in the eggs and cook 1 minute. Turn the heat to medium-low and cover with a lid.
  4. Cook until the egg cake is set except for a little moisture on top, 5 to 7 minutes. Scatter the tomatoes over so they sink slightly, cover again and cook 2 minutes.
  5. Turn off the heat, sprinkle with chives and let it rest, covered, 5 minutes. Cut into wedges and serve with the bacon and rye bread.

Tip: Keep the heat low once the lid goes on; too much heat makes the bottom tough before the top sets. Danes often eat this for a weekend lunch, too.
