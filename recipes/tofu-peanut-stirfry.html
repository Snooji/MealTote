Crispy Tofu & Veggie Peanut Stir-Fry

DinnerThai30 minServes 4750 kcal35 g protein

Photo: Kembangraps · CC BY-SA 3.0 · source

Cornstarch-crisped tofu and colorful vegetables in a creamy peanut-lime sauce over rice. Even tofu skeptics come back for seconds.

Ingredients

  • 28 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 16 oz stir-fry vegetables
  • ⅓ cup peanut butter
  • ¼ cup soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 limes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1½ cups rice
  • crushed peanuts
  • green onions

From your pantry: 3 tbsp cornstarch, 3 tbsp oil, chili flakes, sesame oil.

Instructions

  1. Start the rice. Toss the tofu with the cornstarch and a pinch of salt.
  2. Fry in 2 tbsp oil over medium-high, turning, until crisp on all sides, 10 minutes. Move to a plate.
  3. Whisk the peanut butter, soy, honey, juice of 1 lime, garlic, sesame oil and ⅓ cup hot water.
  4. Stir-fry the vegetables in the remaining oil until hot, 4 minutes. Add the sauce and tofu and toss to coat.
  5. Serve over rice with peanuts, green onions and lime wedges.

Tip: Press the tofu between towels under a heavy pan for 15 minutes. Drier tofu gets crispier.
