PB&J Greek Yogurt Bowls with Banana & Granola

BreakfastAmerican comfort15 minServes 4390 kcal19 g protein

Everyone's favorite sandwich turned breakfast: thick Greek yogurt swirled with peanut butter and a quick warm berry jam, topped with banana and crunchy granola.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups plain Greek yogurt
  • 4 tbsp creamy peanut butter
  • 2 cups frozen mixed berries
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • 1 cup granola

From your pantry: 1 tsp lemon juice, 1 tsp cornstarch.

Steps

  1. Put the berries, honey and lemon juice in a small saucepan over medium heat. Cook, mashing the fruit with a fork, until juicy and bubbling, 5 to 6 minutes.
  2. Stir the cornstarch into 1 tbsp water, add to the berries and simmer 1 minute until thick and jammy. Let cool for a few minutes.
  3. Warm the peanut butter in the microwave for 15 seconds so it drizzles easily.
  4. Spread the yogurt into 4 bowls. Spoon the berry jam on one side, drizzle the peanut butter over the top and swirl lightly with a spoon.
  5. Top with banana slices and granola just before eating so the granola stays crunchy.

Tip: Make a double batch of the berry jam; it keeps a week in the fridge and is great on toast and pancakes too.
