Black Bean & Corn Quesadillas

LunchTex-Mex20 minServes 4750 kcal30 g protein

Photo: nsaum75¡שיחת!‎ · CC BY-SA 3.0 · source

Golden, crisp-edged quesadillas stuffed with seasoned black beans, sweet corn and plenty of melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 cup frozen corn, thawed
  • 2 tsp taco seasoning
  • 2 cups shredded Mexican cheese
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • salsa, for serving
  • sour cream, for serving

From your pantry: 1 tbsp butter or oil.

Instructions

  1. Mash half the beans with a fork and stir in the rest, the corn and the seasoning.
  2. Spread the bean mixture over 4 tortillas, top with cheese and the remaining tortillas.
  3. Cook in a lightly buttered skillet over medium heat until golden underneath, 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Flip carefully and cook until the cheese melts, 2 minutes more.
  5. Cut into wedges and serve with salsa and sour cream.

Tip: Press down with a spatula as they cook. More contact with the pan means crispier edges.
