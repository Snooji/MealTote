Pinchos Morunos (Spiced Pork Skewers) with Roast Potatoes

DinnerSpanish165 minServes 4565 kcal53 g protein

Andalusian tapas-bar skewers of pork marinated in cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, saffron and lemon, seared until charred at the edges and served with crisp roast potatoes and a simple salad.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb pork tenderloin, in 1-inch cubes
  • 2 lemons (juice of 1½, rest in wedges)
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 romaine heart, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • chopped parsley

From your pantry: 6 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp ground coriander, 1 tsp dried oregano, ½ tsp chili powder, ½ tsp turmeric, pinch of saffron, 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Mix 4 tbsp oil, the lemon juice, garlic, all the spices and oregano with some pepper. Toss the pork in it, cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours or overnight. Soak wooden skewers if using.
  2. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp oil and salt and roast cut-side down until golden and tender, 30 to 35 minutes.
  3. Season the pork with 1 tsp salt and thread onto skewers. Heat a grill pan or grill to high (or use the broiler).
  4. Cook the skewers, turning every couple of minutes, until charred in spots and 145°F inside, 8 to 10 minutes total. Rest 3 minutes.
  5. Toss the romaine and tomatoes with the last tbsp oil, the vinegar and salt. Serve the skewers with the potatoes, salad, parsley and lemon wedges.

Tip: Leave the salt out of the marinade if marinating overnight and salt just before cooking, so the meat stays juicy. The Moorish original is made with lamb; pork is the classic Spanish tapas version.
