Turkey Chorizo & Egg-White Freezer Burritos

BreakfastTex-Mex40 minServes 6530 kcal50 g protein

Photo: jeffreyw · CC BY 2.0 · source

Lean ground turkey gets the full chorizo treatment with chili powder, smoked paprika, cumin and a splash of vinegar, then gets scrambled with egg whites and rolled with cheese and salsa. Make a batch on Sunday and grab one each morning.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb 93% lean ground turkey
  • 4 cups (one 32-oz carton) liquid egg whites
  • 4 large eggs
  • ½ yellow onion, finely diced
  • 6 large flour tortillas
  • ¾ cup shredded Mexican-blend cheese
  • ½ cup chunky salsa, plus more for serving
  • ½ bunch cilantro, chopped

From your pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp chili powder, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp garlic powder, ¼ tsp cayenne, 2 tbsp cider vinegar, salt.

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the turkey and onion and cook, breaking the meat into small crumbles, until no pink remains, 6 to 7 minutes.
  2. Stir in the chili powder, paprika, cumin, oregano, garlic powder, cayenne and 1 tsp salt and cook 1 minute until fragrant. Add the vinegar and cook until it evaporates, about 1 minute more. This is your turkey chorizo.
  3. Whisk the egg whites and eggs with a pinch of salt. Lower the heat to medium, pour the eggs over the turkey and gently fold with a spatula until softly set, 3 to 4 minutes. Stir in the cilantro and let the filling cool for 10 minutes.
  4. Warm the tortillas for a few seconds in the microwave so they fold without cracking. Divide the filling down the center of each, top with cheese and a spoonful of salsa, fold in the sides and roll up tightly.
  5. Eat right away, or wrap each burrito in foil or parchment and freeze in a zip-top bag.

Tip: Let the filling cool before rolling. Hot, steamy eggs make soggy tortillas, especially for freezing.
