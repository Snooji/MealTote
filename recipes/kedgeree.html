Kedgeree with Smoked Salmon, Cod & Jammy Eggs

BreakfastBritish & Irish40 minServes 4505 kcal34 g protein

The Anglo-Indian country-house breakfast: buttery curry-spiced rice and red lentils folded with flaked milk-poached cod and smoked salmon, topped with soft-boiled eggs, herbs and lemon.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • ½ cup red lentils
  • 8 oz cod fillet
  • 4 oz smoked salmon (hot-smoked if you can find it), flaked
  • 1 cup milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • a handful of cilantro or parsley, chopped
  • 1 lemon, in wedges

From your pantry: 1 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp curry powder, ½ tsp ground coriander, ¼ tsp ground cardamom, ¼ tsp ground allspice, 1 bay leaf, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice and lentils, put them in a saucepan with the turmeric, ½ tsp salt and 2½ cups water, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low until the water is absorbed, about 12 minutes. Leave covered off the heat.
  2. Meanwhile lower the eggs into boiling water for 7 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
  3. Put the cod, bay leaf and milk in a small pan, bring to a gentle simmer and poach until the fish flakes, 6 to 8 minutes. Lift out and flake, discarding the milk.
  4. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium and cook the onion until soft and starting to brown, 6 minutes. Add the garlic, curry powder, coriander, cardamom and allspice and stir 1 minute.
  5. Fold in the rice and lentils, the cod and the smoked salmon and warm through gently. Season, scatter with herbs and top with halved eggs and lemon wedges.

Tip: Smoked haddock (finnan haddie) is the traditional fish. If you find it, poach it in the milk instead of the cod and skip the smoked salmon.
