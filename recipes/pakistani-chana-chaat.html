Pakistani Chana Chaat with Tamarind & Yogurt

LunchPakistani25 minServes 4435 kcal23 g protein

A tangy, crunchy chickpea and potato salad tossed with onion, tomato, mint and chaat spices, then drizzled with sweet-sour tamarind sauce and cool whipped yogurt. The iftar favorite, built into a full lunch.

Ingredients

  • 3 cans (15.5 oz) chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 lb baby or Yukon gold potatoes
  • 1 small red or yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • ½ cucumber, diced
  • ½ bunch cilantro, chopped
  • a handful of mint leaves, chopped
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 1 lemon

From your pantry: 2 tbsp sugar, 1½ tsp chaat masala, 1 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp red chili flakes, ¼ tsp ground ginger, 1 tsp salt.

Steps

  1. Cover the potatoes with salted water, bring to a boil and cook until tender when pierced, 12 to 15 minutes. Drain, cool a few minutes and cut into bite-size cubes.
  2. Make the tamarind sauce: stir the tamarind paste, 1½ tbsp of the sugar, the ground ginger, a pinch of salt and 3 tbsp hot water together until smooth.
  3. In a big bowl toss the chickpeas, potatoes, onion, tomatoes, cucumber, chili, most of the cilantro and mint, the cumin, coriander, chili flakes, 1 tsp chaat masala, juice of the lemon and ½ tsp salt.
  4. Whisk the yogurt with the remaining sugar, a pinch of salt and 2 tbsp water until smooth and pourable.
  5. Spoon the chaat into bowls, drizzle with yogurt and tamarind sauce, and finish with the remaining herbs and a sprinkle of chaat masala. Serve right away.

Tip: For the authentic crunch, top with sev (crispy chickpea-flour noodles) or crushed papri from a South Asian grocery. Kala namak (black salt) adds the signature eggy, sulfurous tang.
