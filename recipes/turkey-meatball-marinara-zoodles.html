Turkey Meatballs Marinara over Zucchini Noodles & Spaghetti

DinnerItalian45 minServes 4635 kcal64 g protein

Tender Parmesan turkey meatballs baked and simmered in marinara, served over a half-and-half tangle of zucchini noodles and spaghetti under melty mozzarella. Spaghetti night with twice the protein.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 93% lean ground turkey
  • 1 lb 99% lean ground turkey
  • ¼ cup liquid egg whites
  • ⅓ cup panko
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan, divided
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • ¼ cup chopped parsley
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara
  • 3 zucchini, spiralized
  • 3 oz spaghetti
  • ½ cup shredded mozzarella
  • fresh basil

From your pantry: 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, ¼ tsp red pepper flakes, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a sheet pan with parchment. Mix both turkeys with the egg whites, panko, ⅓ cup Parmesan, garlic, parsley, Italian seasoning, 1 tsp salt and pepper. Roll into 20 meatballs.
  2. Bake until browned and 165°F inside, 16 to 18 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile bring the marinara and red pepper flakes to a simmer in a wide skillet. Add the baked meatballs and simmer gently 5 minutes.
  4. Cook the spaghetti in salted water. Sauté the zucchini noodles in the olive oil over medium-high heat with a pinch of salt just until tender, 2 to 3 minutes, then toss with the drained spaghetti.
  5. Scatter the mozzarella over the meatballs, cover until melted, and serve over the noodles with basil and the rest of the Parmesan.

Tip: Don't salt the zucchini noodles until they're in the pan, and cook them hot and fast; that keeps them from going watery.
