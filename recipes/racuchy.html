Racuchy (Polish Yeasted Apple Pancakes)

BreakfastPolish85 minServes 5340 kcal6 g protein

Photo: Wikited at English Wikipedia · Public domain · source

Puffy, golden yeast pancakes studded with cinnamon apples and dusted with powdered sugar. Polish grandmothers' Sunday breakfast, crisp-edged and pillowy inside.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ cups warm milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 apples (1 lb), peeled and diced or thinly sliced
  • ½ cup sour cream or yogurt, to serve

From your pantry: 2½ cups flour, 2¼ tsp instant yeast, 3 tbsp sugar, ½ tsp salt, 1 tsp cinnamon, ⅓ cup oil for frying, 2 tbsp powdered sugar.

Instructions

  1. Stir the yeast and 1 tbsp sugar into the warm milk (about 105°F) and let it foam, 10 minutes.
  2. Add the flour, eggs, remaining sugar and salt and beat with a spoon into a thick, sticky batter, about 2 minutes. Cover and leave in a warm spot until doubled and bubbly, about 1 hour.
  3. Toss the apples with the cinnamon and gently fold them into the risen batter without stirring out all the air.
  4. Heat a thin layer of oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Drop heaped spoonfuls of batter, spread slightly, and fry until deep golden, 2 to 3 minutes per side; they should puff and cook through. Add oil between batches as needed and drain on paper towels.
  5. Dust generously with powdered sugar and serve warm with sour cream.

Tip: Keep the heat at medium: too hot and the outsides brown before the thick centers cook. Mix the batter the night before and let it rise slowly in the fridge for a faster morning.
