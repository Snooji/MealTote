French Onion Chicken

DinnerFrench50 minServes 4760 kcal61 g protein

Everything you love about French onion soup over seared chicken: deeply caramelized onions, a rich beef-thyme sauce and bubbling Gruyère.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken breasts, halved into cutlets
  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 6 oz Gruyère, shredded
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • thyme or parsley
  • crusty baguette, to serve

From your pantry: 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp thyme, 1 tbsp flour, splash of dry sherry or balsamic, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Cook the onions in 3 tbsp butter with a pinch of salt over medium-low, stirring often, until deep golden and jammy, 25 to 30 minutes.
  2. Season the chicken and sear in the oil and remaining butter, 4 minutes per side. Set aside.
  3. Add the garlic, thyme and flour to the onions for 1 minute, then deglaze with sherry and the broth. Simmer until thickened.
  4. Return the chicken, spoon onions over each piece and cover with Gruyère.
  5. Broil 2 to 3 minutes until bubbling and browned. Serve with baguette.

Tip: Low and slow onions are worth it. Add a splash of water if they start to stick.
