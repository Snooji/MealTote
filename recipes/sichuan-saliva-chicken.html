Saliva Chicken (Sichuan Chicken in Red Chili Oil)

LunchSichuan & Hunan40 minServes 4690 kcal44 g protein

Kou shui ji, "mouthwatering chicken": silky poached chicken drenched in a numbing, garlicky chili oil sauce with tangy vinegar and crushed peanuts. Served cool over rice with crisp cucumber, it's a lunch you'll crave.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless chicken thighs
  • 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 4 slices ginger plus 1 tsp grated
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • ¼ cup chili crisp or chili oil
  • ½ tsp ground Sichuan peppercorns
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp creamy peanut butter
  • ¼ cup roasted peanuts, chopped
  • 1 English cucumber, smashed and cut in chunks
  • 1½ cups jasmine rice

From your pantry: 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tsp sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp salt.

Steps

  1. Start the rice. Put the chicken in a pot with the ginger slices, Shaoxing wine, salt and cold water to cover by an inch. Bring to a bare simmer over medium, then cover and cook on low until it reaches 165°F inside, 12 to 15 minutes.
  2. Move the chicken to a bowl of ice water for 5 minutes so it stays juicy and firm, then pat dry. Save ¼ cup of the poaching liquid.
  3. Whisk the soy, vinegar, sugar, peanut butter, sesame oil, grated garlic and ginger, Sichuan pepper, the white parts of the green onions and 3 tbsp poaching liquid until smooth.
  4. Toss the cucumber with a pinch of salt. Slice the chicken into ½-inch strips and lay it on a platter.
  5. Pour the sauce over the chicken, spoon the chili crisp on top and scatter with peanuts and green onion tops. Serve with the rice and cucumber.

Tip: Chinkiang black vinegar gives the sauce its true malty tang. Use it in place of rice vinegar if you can find it.
