Sweet Corn Griddle Cakes with Maple Butter & Turkey Bacon

BreakfastSouthern & soul food35 minServes 4590 kcal17 g protein

Photo: City Foodsters · CC BY 2.0 · source

Golden cornmeal pancakes studded with sweet corn and green onion, crisp at the edges and fluffy inside, served with maple butter and a side of turkey bacon.

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup yellow cornmeal
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1¼ cups milk
  • 5 tbsp butter, divided
  • 1 cup frozen corn, thawed
  • 3 green onions, thinly sliced
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • 8 slices turkey bacon

From your pantry: 1 cup flour, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp baking powder, ½ tsp salt, 1 tbsp oil.

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, cornmeal, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl. In another bowl whisk the eggs, milk and 3 tbsp melted butter.
  2. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir just until combined; a few lumps are fine. Fold in the corn and green onions and let the batter rest 5 minutes.
  3. Cook the turkey bacon in a large skillet over medium heat until crisp, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Keep warm in a low oven.
  4. Wipe the skillet, brush it with oil and set over medium heat. Scoop ¼ cup batter per cake and cook until bubbles form and the edges look dry, about 2½ minutes. Flip and cook until golden, 1½ to 2 minutes more.
  5. Stir the remaining 2 tbsp softened butter into the maple syrup. Serve the cakes stacked with a spoonful of maple butter and the turkey bacon on the side.

Tip: Pat the thawed corn dry on a towel before folding it in. Wet kernels thin the batter and keep the cakes from browning.
