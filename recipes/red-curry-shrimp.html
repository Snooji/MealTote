Coconut Red Curry with Shrimp

DinnerThai25 minServes 4820 kcal35 g protein

Photo: Takeaway · CC BY-SA 3.0 · source

A fast, glossy Thai-style red curry with sweet peppers, spinach and shrimp, ladled over jasmine rice and brightened with lime.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled
  • 3 tbsp red curry paste (or most of a jar of red curry sauce)
  • 2 cans coconut milk
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 big handfuls baby spinach
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (or fish sauce)
  • 2 limes
  • a handful of cilantro
  • 1½ cups jasmine rice

From your pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp sugar.

Steps

  1. Start the rice. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high and cook the onion and peppers until just softened, 4 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and curry paste and fry, stirring, until fragrant and brick-red, 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the coconut milk, soy sauce and sugar. Simmer 5 minutes until slightly thickened.
  4. Add the shrimp and simmer 3 to 4 minutes, just until pink. Stir in the spinach to wilt.
  5. Finish with the juice of 1 lime and taste for salt. Serve over rice with cilantro and lime wedges.

Tip: Red curry paste varies a lot in heat. Start with 2 tbsp and add more after tasting the sauce.
