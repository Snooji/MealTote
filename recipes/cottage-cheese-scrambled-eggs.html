Creamy Cottage Cheese Scrambled Eggs with Canadian Bacon

BreakfastAmerican comfort15 minServes 4390 kcal39 g protein

Photo: avlxyz · CC BY-SA 2.0 · source

Cottage cheese whisked into eggs melts into the softest, creamiest scramble you've made, with twice the protein. Served with seared Canadian bacon, juicy tomatoes and a slice of sourdough toast.

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 1½ cups liquid egg whites
  • 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 6 oz Canadian bacon (about 12 slices)
  • 1 cup grape tomatoes, halved
  • 2 green onions or a few chives, sliced
  • 4 thin slices sourdough

From your pantry: 2 tsp butter, ¼ tsp salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs, egg whites, cottage cheese, salt and pepper in a bowl. Toast the sourdough.
  2. Sear the Canadian bacon in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until browned at the edges, about 1 minute per side. Move to plates.
  3. Lower the heat to medium-low and melt the butter. Pour in the eggs and let them sit until the edges just begin to set, then slowly push them toward the center with a spatula, letting big soft curds form, 3 to 5 minutes.
  4. Take the pan off the heat while they still look a little wet; they finish cooking on the way to the plate.
  5. Serve with the Canadian bacon, tomatoes, toast and a scatter of green onions.

Tip: Low heat matters most here. Cottage cheese weeps and turns the eggs watery if they boil, so stir gently and pull them early.
