Samke Harra (Spicy Baked Cod with Warm Tahini Sauce & Walnuts)

DinnerLebanese & Levantine40 minServes 4820 kcal54 g protein

Tripoli's famous spiced fish: cod fillets roasted with cumin and chili, smothered in a lemony tahini sauce cooked with onion, garlic and cilantro, and showered with toasted walnuts. Served over rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb cod fillets, patted dry
  • ½ cup tahini
  • 2 lemons, juiced (about ⅓ cup)
  • 1 yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
  • ½ cup walnuts, roughly chopped
  • 1½ cups basmati rice

From your pantry: 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder or ¼ tsp cayenne, ½ tsp white pepper, salt.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and start the rice. Lay the cod on a parchment-lined sheet, brush with 2 tbsp oil and season with the cumin, chili, white pepper and 1 tsp salt.
  2. Roast the fish until it is opaque and flakes easily, 15 to 20 minutes depending on thickness.
  3. Meanwhile, warm the remaining oil in a small saucepan over medium heat and cook the onion until soft and translucent, 5 to 7 minutes. Add the garlic and most of the cilantro and cook 1 minute.
  4. Whisk in the tahini, lemon juice and ½ cup water. It will look curdled at first; keep stirring over low heat until smooth and pourable, 2 to 3 minutes, adding water a spoonful at a time if it gets too thick. Season with salt.
  5. Toast the walnuts in a dry skillet over medium heat until fragrant, 3 to 4 minutes.
  6. Spoon the warm sauce over the fish, scatter with walnuts and the rest of the cilantro, and serve with the rice.

Tip: Pine nuts or almonds fried in a little oil are the traditional topping; walnuts are what the old Tripoli recipes use too and cost much less. Add a chopped fresh chili to the onions if you like real heat.
