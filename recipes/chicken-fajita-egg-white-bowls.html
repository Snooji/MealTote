Chicken Fajita Egg-White Scramble Bowls

BreakfastTex-Mex30 minServes 4445 kcal52 g protein

Sizzling chili-lime chicken and charred peppers and onions, folded into soft egg whites and piled over black beans with salsa, avocado and a little cheese. Breakfast with serious fajita-night energy.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, in thin strips
  • 3 cups liquid egg whites
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • ½ onion, sliced
  • ½ can black beans, rinsed and warmed
  • ½ cup chunky salsa
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 6 tbsp shredded Mexican-blend cheese
  • a handful of cilantro, chopped
  • 1 lime

From your pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt.

Instructions

  1. Toss the chicken with the chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, ¾ tsp salt and the juice of half the lime.
  2. Heat 2 tsp of the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken in one layer until browned and cooked through, 165°F, 5 to 6 minutes. Move to a plate.
  3. Add the peppers and onion to the pan and cook until softened and charred at the edges, 5 to 6 minutes. Return the chicken to the pan.
  4. Push everything to one side, lower the heat to medium-low, add the rest of the oil and pour the egg whites into the empty side with a pinch of salt. Stir gently until just set, 3 to 4 minutes, then fold everything together.
  5. Spoon the black beans into 4 bowls, top with the fajita scramble, cheese, salsa, avocado and cilantro, and serve with the remaining lime in wedges.

Tip: Don't crowd the chicken. Strips that touch steam instead of browning, so cook in two batches if your skillet is small.
