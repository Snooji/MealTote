Broccoli Cheddar Soup with Crusty Bread

LunchAmerican comfort35 minServes 4715 kcal32 g protein

Thick, velvety, cafe-style broccoli soup loaded with sharp cheddar and a little carrot, with warm sourdough on the side for dunking.

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lb broccoli, chopped small
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
  • ½ loaf sourdough, sliced

From your pantry: ¼ cup flour, ½ tsp dry mustard, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp pepper, pinch of nutmeg.

Steps

  1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes, then add the garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Sprinkle in the flour and stir constantly for 2 minutes to cook out the raw taste.
  3. Slowly whisk in the broth, then the milk, until smooth. Add the broccoli, carrot, dry mustard, salt, pepper and nutmeg and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Simmer, stirring often, until the broccoli is very tender and the soup has thickened, 15 to 18 minutes. Blend about a third of it with an immersion blender if you like it thicker.
  5. Turn off the heat and stir in the cheddar a handful at a time until melted and smooth. Serve with the sourdough.

Tip: Take the pot off the heat before adding the cheese and use block cheddar you shred yourself if you can. Boiling makes cheese grainy.
