Jamaican Beef Patties with Flaky Turmeric Crust

LunchJamaican90 minServes 6670 kcal21 g protein

Photo: ritcharnd moskow from Toshi Station, USA · CC BY-SA 2.0 · source

Golden half-moon pastries with a buttery, flaky turmeric crust wrapped around spicy, thyme-scented curried beef. A dozen patties that freeze like a dream for grab-and-go lunches.

Ingredients

  • 2½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup cold butter, cubed
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 1 Scotch bonnet or 2 jalapeños, minced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup panko
  • ¾ cup beef broth
  • 1 egg, beaten

From your pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 3 tsp curry powder, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp dried thyme, ½ tsp allspice, ½ cup ice water, 1 tsp sugar, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Make the dough: whisk the flour, 1 tsp curry powder, the turmeric, sugar and 1 tsp salt. Cut in the cold butter with your fingers until it looks like coarse crumbs with some pea-size bits. Stir in ½ cup ice water a little at a time until it holds together, then flatten into a disk, wrap and chill 30 minutes.
  2. Make the filling: heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high and cook the onion, green onions, chile and garlic until soft, 3 to 4 minutes. Add the beef, the rest of the curry powder, the thyme, allspice, ½ tsp salt and pepper and cook, breaking it up, until browned, 6 to 7 minutes.
  3. Add the broth and panko and simmer until thick and moist but not wet, 3 to 4 minutes. Spread on a plate to cool completely.
  4. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a sheet pan with parchment. Divide the dough into 12 pieces and roll each into a 5½-inch circle about ⅛ inch thick.
  5. Spoon a heaping ¼ cup filling onto half of each circle, brush the edge with egg, fold over and crimp with a fork. Brush the tops with egg.
  6. Bake until golden and flaky, 25 to 28 minutes. Cool 5 minutes before eating; the filling is very hot.

Tip: Make sure the filling is completely cool before you fill the dough, or the butter melts and the crust won't flake.
