Cuban Mojo Chicken Rice Bowls with Black Beans & Pineapple

LunchCuban45 minServes 4645 kcal67 g protein

Chicken soaked in a garlicky orange-lime mojo with mint and oregano, seared until caramelized and piled over rice and cumin black beans with pineapple, avocado and cilantro.

Ingredients

  • 2¼ lb boneless chicken breasts
  • 1 orange, juiced (about ⅓ cup)
  • 2 limes
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • a handful of fresh mint
  • ¼ red onion, finely diced
  • ¾ cup rice
  • 1 can black beans, with liquid
  • 1½ cups diced pineapple
  • ½ avocado, diced
  • ½ bunch cilantro, chopped

From your pantry: 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp cumin, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Blend the orange juice, juice of 1 lime, garlic, mint, oregano, half the cumin, 1 tbsp oil and 1 tsp salt into a mojo. Pour half over the chicken and marinate 20 minutes or up to overnight; save the rest.
  2. Cook the rice. Simmer the black beans with their liquid, the remaining cumin and a pinch of salt until thick, 8 to 10 minutes.
  3. Heat the remaining oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken until browned and 165°F inside, 6 to 7 minutes per side. Rest 5 minutes and slice.
  4. Toss the pineapple with the red onion, half the cilantro and a squeeze of lime.
  5. Build bowls with rice, beans and chicken, spoon the reserved mojo over the chicken and top with pineapple, avocado and cilantro.

Tip: Traditional mojo uses sour (bitter) orange; a mix of orange and lime juice is the standard stand-in and tastes very close.
