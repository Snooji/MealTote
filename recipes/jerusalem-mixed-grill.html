Jerusalem Mixed Grill (Spiced Chicken & Onions in Pita with Tahini)

LunchIsraeli35 minServes 4590 kcal43 g protein

Photo: Peterjerusalem · CC BY-SA 4.0 · source

Mahane Yehuda market's late-night favorite, made with all thigh meat: chicken and onions seared hard on a flat-top with cumin, turmeric and baharat spices, stuffed into pita with tahini, quick-pickled onion and salad.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless chicken thighs, in thin strips
  • 2 yellow onions (1½ sliced for cooking, ½ for pickling)
  • 4 pitas
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon
  • ½ cucumber, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • parsley, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced

From your pantry: 3 tbsp olive oil, ¼ cup white vinegar, ½ tsp sugar, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp ground cardamom, ¼ tsp allspice, salt, pepper.

Instructions

  1. Stir the vinegar, sugar and ¼ tsp salt until dissolved, add the thinly sliced half onion and set aside to pickle.
  2. Whisk the tahini with 1 tbsp lemon juice, a pinch of salt and 4 to 5 tbsp water until creamy.
  3. Toss the chicken with the spices, garlic, 1 tsp salt, pepper and 1 tbsp oil.
  4. Heat the rest of the oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add the sliced onions and cook until charred at the edges, 4 minutes, then add the chicken in a single layer and stir only every 2 to 3 minutes so it browns, until cooked through and crusty, 8 to 10 minutes.
  5. Squeeze the remaining lemon over the chicken. Stuff into warm pitas with the cucumber, tomato, parsley and pickled onion, and spoon over the tahini.

Tip: Traditionally this includes chicken hearts and livers; add ½ lb of livers in the last 3 minutes if you like them. A nonstick or cast-iron pan as hot as you can get it mimics the street-stand griddle.
