Creamy Tuna Noodle Casserole with Peas

DinnerAmerican comfort45 minServes 6630 kcal36 g protein

The retro classic made from scratch: wide egg noodles, tuna, peas and mushrooms in a creamy Parmesan sauce, baked under a crunchy cheddar-panko topping.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz wide egg noodles
  • 1½ cups frozen peas
  • 8 oz sliced mushrooms
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • 3 cans (5 oz each) tuna, drained
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • ½ cup panko

From your pantry: ¼ cup flour, ½ tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, 1 tbsp oil.

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Cook the noodles in salted water 2 minutes short of the package time, adding the peas for the last minute. Drain.
  2. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat and cook the onion and mushrooms until soft and the liquid has cooked off, 7 to 8 minutes.
  3. Sprinkle in the flour and stir 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the milk and broth and simmer, stirring, until thick, 3 to 4 minutes. Off the heat, stir in the sour cream, Parmesan, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  4. Fold in the noodles, peas and flaked tuna and spread in a 9x13-inch dish. Toss the panko with the oil and cheddar and scatter over the top.
  5. Bake until bubbling and golden, 20 to 25 minutes. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

Tip: Undercook the noodles slightly; they keep softening in the sauce in the oven and won't turn mushy.
