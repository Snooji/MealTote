Maple-Sage Turkey Sausage Patties with Egg-White Scramble & Toast

BreakfastAmerican comfort30 minServes 4430 kcal46 g protein

Photo: diettogo1 · CC BY-SA 2.0 · source

Homemade breakfast sausage patties that taste like the real deal, sweet with maple and fragrant with sage and thyme, served with a soft egg-white scramble, whole-wheat toast and fresh strawberries.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lb 93% lean ground turkey
  • 2 cups liquid egg whites
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 4 slices whole-wheat bread
  • 8 oz strawberries, hulled

From your pantry: 1 tbsp maple syrup, 1 tsp dried sage, ½ tsp dried thyme, ½ tsp garlic powder, ¼ tsp red pepper flakes, 2 tsp oil, salt, pepper.

Instructions

  1. Mix the turkey, maple syrup, sage, thyme, garlic powder, pepper flakes, 1 tsp salt and ¼ tsp pepper with your hands just until combined; overmixing makes them dense. Shape into 8 thin patties with damp hands.
  2. Heat 1 tsp of the oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Cook the patties until well browned, 4 to 5 minutes, then flip, press lightly and cook 3 to 4 minutes more, to 165°F inside. Keep warm on a plate.
  3. Wipe the pan, add the remaining oil and lower the heat to medium-low. Whisk the egg whites and eggs with a pinch of salt, pour in and stir gently until soft curds form, 3 to 4 minutes. Fold in the green onions.
  4. Toast the bread and serve each plate with 2 patties, eggs, toast and strawberries.

Tip: Mix the patty seasoning the night before and refrigerate the mixture; the sage and maple flavors soak in. Raw patties also freeze well between sheets of parchment.
