Curry Chicken Salad Lettuce Cups with Apple & Cashews

LunchAmerican comfort30 minServes 4510 kcal63 g protein

Photo: TheHungryDudes · CC BY 2.0 · source

Tender poached chicken in a golden curry-yogurt dressing with crisp apple, celery and toasted cashews, spooned into butter lettuce with warm pita on the side. Sweet, savory and very packable.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 apple, finely diced
  • 3 celery ribs, diced
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 3 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • ⅓ cup roasted cashews, chopped
  • 2 heads butter lettuce
  • 2 pitas, warmed and cut in wedges

From your pantry: 1 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp honey, ½ tsp cumin, ¼ tsp turmeric, ¼ tsp cayenne, salt, pepper.

Instructions

  1. Put the chicken in a pot, cover with cold water by an inch, add 1 tsp salt and bring to a gentle simmer. Cover, turn off the heat and let it sit until 165°F inside, 15 to 18 minutes.
  2. Let the chicken cool a few minutes, then dice or shred it.
  3. Whisk the yogurt, mayo, honey, lemon juice, curry powder, cumin, turmeric, cayenne, ½ tsp salt and pepper in a large bowl.
  4. Fold in the chicken, apple, celery, green onions and cilantro. Chill at least 15 minutes so the curry flavor blooms.
  5. Spoon into lettuce cups, scatter with cashews and serve with warm pita wedges.

Tip: Poaching off the heat keeps lean breast meat juicy; boiling it hard is what makes chicken salad dry and stringy.
