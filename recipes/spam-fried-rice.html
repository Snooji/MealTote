Garlicky Spam & Egg Fried Rice

BreakfastHawaiian20 minServes 4670 kcal25 g protein

Hawai'i's go-to local breakfast: crisped Spam, a whole head of garlic and cold rice pressed into the pan until golden, tossed with soft scrambled eggs and green onion.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (12 oz) Spam, diced
  • 5 cups cold cooked rice (from 1½ cups raw)
  • 5 eggs
  • 1 whole head garlic, minced
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce or Maggi

From your pantry: 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp sesame oil, black pepper.

Steps

  1. Fry the Spam in a large dry nonstick skillet or wok over medium-high, stirring now and then, until browned and a little crisp, 5 to 6 minutes. Move it to a bowl.
  2. Add the oil and the garlic and stir until pale golden and fragrant, about 1 minute. Watch it closely; burnt garlic turns bitter.
  3. Add the rice, breaking up clumps, and toss with the garlic. Press it flat and leave it alone until the bottom crisps, 3 to 4 minutes, then stir and repeat once.
  4. Push the rice to the sides, crack the eggs into the center with half the soy sauce and scramble until just set, about 1 minute.
  5. Toss everything together with the Spam, remaining soy, sesame oil, green onions and plenty of black pepper. Serve hot.

Tip: Maggi seasoning is the local secret; use it in place of soy if you can find it. Swap half the Spam for Portuguese sausage for the other classic island version.
