Baked Ham & Cheddar Egg Cups

BreakfastAmerican comfort25 minServes 4410 kcal44 g protein

Photo: jeffreyw · CC BY 2.0 · source

Sliced ham pressed into muffin cups becomes a crisp-edged little bowl for a whole egg and a pinch of sharp cheddar. Bake them runny or firm, and eat three with hot sauce.

Ingredients

  • 18 oz thinly sliced deli ham (24 slices)
  • 12 large eggs
  • 6 tbsp shredded sharp cheddar
  • 2 green onions, sliced

From your pantry: cooking spray, salt, pepper.

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F and coat a 12-cup muffin pan generously with cooking spray.
  2. Press 2 overlapping slices of ham into each cup to line the bottom and sides, letting the edges stand up above the rim.
  3. Sprinkle about 1½ tsp cheddar into each, then crack an egg into each cup and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Bake until the whites are set: about 12 minutes for runny yolks, 15 for firm.
  5. Let them sit 3 minutes, then lift out with a spoon, sprinkle with green onions and serve 3 per person.

Tip: Bake them firm for meal prep. Runny-yolk cups don't reheat well, while set ones keep 4 days and microwave in 45 seconds.
