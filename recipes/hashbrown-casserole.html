Sausage & Cheddar Hash Brown Breakfast Bake

BreakfastAmerican comfort60 minServes 8505 kcal26 g protein

A crowd-pleasing make-ahead bake of crispy hash browns, browned sausage, eggs and sharp cheddar. Assemble the night before, bake in the morning.

Ingredients

  • 30 oz frozen shredded hash browns, thawed
  • 1 lb breakfast sausage
  • 10 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar
  • 4 green onions
  • 1 bell pepper, diced

From your pantry: Butter, salt, pepper, hot sauce.

Steps

  1. Brown the sausage with the pepper, breaking it up. Drain.
  2. Butter a 9x13 and press in the hash browns. Season with salt and pepper and bake at 425°F for 20 minutes to crisp the base.
  3. Scatter over the sausage, most of the cheese and green onions.
  4. Whisk the eggs, milk, ½ tsp salt and pepper and pour over. Top with the remaining cheese.
  5. Lower the oven to 375°F and bake 30 to 35 minutes, until set in the center. Rest 10 minutes.

Tip: To make ahead, assemble without baking the hash browns first, refrigerate overnight and bake 50 minutes at 375°F.
