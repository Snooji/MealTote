Blueberry Cottage Cheese Blender Pancakes

BreakfastAmerican comfort25 minServes 4470 kcal46 g protein

Photo: kae71463 · CC BY 2.0 · source

Everything goes into the blender: cottage cheese, oats, eggs and a little vanilla protein. The result is fluffy, custardy pancakes studded with blueberries, served with a dollop of Greek yogurt.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1½ cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup liquid egg whites
  • 2 scoops vanilla whey protein powder
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt, for serving

From your pantry: 2 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp cinnamon, 2 tsp vanilla extract, 1 tbsp butter, 2 tbsp maple syrup, salt.

Steps

  1. Add the cottage cheese, eggs, egg whites, vanilla, oats, protein powder, baking powder, cinnamon and a pinch of salt to a blender. Blend on high until smooth, about 45 seconds.
  2. Let the batter rest 5 minutes so the oats absorb the liquid and it thickens.
  3. Heat a nonstick griddle or skillet over medium heat and brush lightly with butter. Pour ¼ cup batter per pancake and scatter a few blueberries on top.
  4. Cook until the edges look set and a few bubbles appear, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook 1 minute more until golden. Don't press them down.
  5. Serve stacked with Greek yogurt and a drizzle of maple syrup.

Tip: Keep the heat at medium, not higher. The cottage cheese and protein make these brown faster than regular pancakes, so they can scorch before the middle sets.
